  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的Root-MUSIC性能分析大多依赖于渐近假设，且通常忽略了干扰根对算法结果的潜在影响。本文旨在移除这些假设，提供更严谨、更具现实指导意义的非渐近理论保证。

Method: 通过分析Root-MUSIC多项式的几何性质，证明了所有干扰根（extraneous roots）均位于单位圆附近的特定环形区域之外，从而在无需“假设干扰根不被选中”的前提下，导出了关于模型参数的显式非渐近误差界限。

Result: 研究证明了Root-MUSIC在单快照和多快照模型下的频率估计误差界限。特别是在多快照模型中，误差界限为 $O(\sigma/(m \sqrt{n}))$，明确揭示了增加传感器数量（$m$）能显著降低误差，这一结果通过数值模拟得到了验证。

Conclusion: 该研究确立了Root-MUSIC算法的非渐近误差界限，通过几何性质证明了干扰根远离单位圆，从而验证了算法在频率估计中的鲁棒性和高精度，并量化了传感器数量对性能提升的贡献。

Abstract: Root-MUSIC is a spectral estimation algorithm that approximates the unknown signal frequencies by constructing a high-degree polynomial and finding a subset of roots which are closest to the complex unit circle. Previous works found asymptotic expectation formulas for the performance of Root-MUSIC under the implicit assumption that the aforementioned root selection criterion does not select extraneous roots -- those which are unrelated to the correct parameters. This paper removes the need for this assumption by showing all extraneous roots lie outside an annulus of a certain thickness and therefore are not selected by the algorithm. This paper also provides sharp, non-asymptotic, and explicit error bounds for the correct roots in terms of fundamental model parameters. All results hold under a natural separation condition on the correct signal frequencies and are applicable in both the single- and multi-snapshot models. More specifically, in the multi-snapshot model, we prove that Root-MUSIC estimates the frequencies with error at most $O(σ/(m \sqrt n))$, where $σ^2$ is the noise variance, $m$ is the number of sensors, and $n$ is the number of snapshots. A novelty of this non-asymptotic bound is the explicit $1/m$ decay, which indicates that there is a significant advantage in utilizing additional sensors. Numerical simulations confirm our theory. The main mathematical insight of this paper is a geometric property of the Root-MUSIC polynomial: its correct roots are highly stable to noise while its extraneous roots must lie outside of an annulus.
