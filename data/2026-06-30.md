Method: 该研究将接收机选择问题转化为有限信噪比下的模态可恢复性问题，提出了三个嵌套的可恢复性准则（模态自由度、联合目标子空间、存在干扰模态下的目标模态），并推导了基于Schur补的误差评估方法，对比了不同贪婪选择规则的性能。

Result: 数值结果表明，在较低可靠性要求下，全局log-det准则更具效率；而在高可靠性要求（严格指标）下，基于Schur补的选择规则表现出更优的有效性。此外，研究还给出了判定可靠性目标是否可达的理论测试方法。

Conclusion: 本文提出的基于Schur补的接收机选择框架在处理高可靠性需求时优于传统的贪婪算法，为大规模天线系统中的子集选择提供了理论支撑和实用准则。

Abstract: Large-aperture wave receivers can contain a large number of candidate sensor locations, antenna ports, or measurement blocks, while hardware and processing constraints allow only a subset to be activated. In this paper, receiver selection is formulated as a finite-SNR modal-recoverability problem, where the selected subset is required to support reliable recovery in every direction of a prescribed modal subspace. However, large trace, log-det, or codebook-distance values alone do not ensure that every prescribed modal direction is resolved. Specifically, the proposed framework consists of three nested recoverability criteria for individual modal degrees, a joint target subspace, and target modes in the presence of active non-target modes. Numerical results demonstrate that global log-det is generally more access-efficient at moderate reliability floors, whereas Schur-based selection is more effective at stringent floors. While this paper is motivated by holographic and XL-MIMO receivers, the framework can be applied to general sampled wave systems.
