Result: 在单频段分析中，CI模型相比FI模型更具物理意义且参数稳定性更高；在多频段分析中，CI和CIF模型表现出优异的跨频段稳定性，其距离指数与3GPP标准高度一致。相比之下，ABG模型虽然在拟合误差上有极小的降低，但伴随显著的参数敏感性，因此推荐采用更具物理鲁棒性的CI/CIF模型。

Conclusion: 本研究证实，基于1米近端自由空间路径损耗锚点的CI和CIF模型，能够有效地将现有的3GPP UMi路径损耗模型扩展至0.5-150 GHz的超宽频段，且具有良好的物理可解释性和稳定性。

Abstract: Extending standardized urban microcell (UMi) path loss (PL) models from the upper mid-band (FR3) to sub-terahertz (sub-THz) frequencies requires measurement-based comparisons spanning a wide spectral range within a unified framework. In this paper, we derive single- and multi-frequency omnidirectional PL models for UMi environments based on extensive NYU WIRELESS measurements at 6.75, 16.95, 28, 73, and 142 GHz in line-of-sight (LOS) and non-line-of-sight (NLOS) conditions. Single-frequency close-in (CI) and floating-intercept (FI) models for each band show that CI yields more stable and physically interpretable parameters, with shadow fading standard deviations within 0.98 dB of FI across all five frequencies. We then extend the multi-frequency PL analysis to the 7-24 GHz, 0.5-100 GHz, and 0.5-150 GHz frequency ranges using CI, close-in with frequency weighting (CIF), and alpha-beta-gamma (ABG) models. Across progressively wider frequency spans, CI and CIF maintain stable distance exponents that remain closely aligned with the 3rd Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) UMi interpretations, whereas ABG offers only very modest reductions in fitting error at the cost of much greater parameter sensitivity. These results support physically anchored CI/CIF formulations with a close-in free space path loss anchor point at 1 m in order to extend 3GPP-oriented UMi PL models over the entire 0.5 to 150 GHz frequency range.
