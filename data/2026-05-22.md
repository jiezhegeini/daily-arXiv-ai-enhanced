### [1] [Rapid Adaptive Matched Filter for Detecting Radar Targets with Unknown Velocity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21685)
*Anatolii A. Kononov*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于可能目标检测区域的多普勒域局部化自适应匹配滤波器雷达目标检测方法，在强非均匀杂波且训练数据有限的环境中实现快速近最优检测，无需杂波频谱参数且性能优于传统GLR方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有雷达目标检测方法在非均匀杂波环境中需要杂波频谱参数，当目标多普勒频率未知时性能显著下降，亟需一种无需先验信息且鲁棒性强的检测方案。

Method: 提出RPTD-based DDL-AMF检测器，利用可能目标检测区域（捕获目标信号功率主要部分的多普勒单元集合）进行自适应匹配滤波，避免了传统RODI方法所需的杂波参数测量和检测改善区域定位。

Result: 相比RODI-based DDL-GLR检测器，所提方法在目标多普勒频率未知和多模杂波谱条件下，实现了接近最优的快速自适应检测性能，且完全无需任何杂波频谱参数信息。

Conclusion: 该检测器在强杂波环境下展现出显著优势，为有限训练数据场景提供了实用高效的雷达目标检测解决方案，具有重要应用价值。

Abstract: This paper introduces a Doppler domain localized (DDL) implementation of the adaptive matched filter (AMF) for radar target detection in severely heterogeneous clutter environments with limited training data. The proposed detector uses the concept of a region of possible target detection (RPTD), a small set of Doppler bins that capture most of the target signal power. This RPTD-based DDL-AMF detector outperforms an earlier suggested DDL implementation of the generalized likelihood ratio (GLR) test, which employs the region of detection improvement (RODI) concept. Unlike the RODI-based DDL-GLR detector, the proposed DDL-AMF detector requires no information on clutter spectrum parameters and no measurements to determine the number and locations of RODIs. Moreover, the performance of the RODI-based DDL-GLR detector falls far below the optimum when the target Doppler frequency is unknown. In contrast, the RPTD-based DDL-AMF detector ensures rapid adaptive detection with near-optimum performance under unknown target Doppler frequency and multimodal clutter spectra.

### [2] [Automated Detection of Urological Events in Bladder Pressure Signals with a Two-Stage Machine Learning Framework Validated on External Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21878)
*Hassaan A. Bukhari,Vikram Abbaraju,Jay Patel,Becky Clarkson,Shachi Tyagi,Margot S. Damaser,Steve J. A. Majerus*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本研究开发了一个机器学习框架，仅从单通道膀胱压力信号中自动识别三类泌尿事件（腹压、逼尿肌过度活动、排尿收缩），两阶段模型准确率达84-90%，为无线家用监测设备提供了算法基础。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统尿动力学检查需要侵入性双导管置入且依赖人工标注事件，而新兴无线无导管技术仅能提供原始膀胱压力数据，缺乏自动化分析能力。为实现居家动态监测，需开发从单通道压力信号中自动识别关键泌尿事件的算法。

Method: 分析118例标注尿动力学曲线，将数据切分为0.8秒片段，经离散小波变换提取每段55维统计特征。将23.3万个同类别连续片段聚合成事件，并采用中位数聚合得到事件级表征。使用两阶段多层感知器处理类别不平衡数据：第一阶段二分类（排尿收缩 vs 非排尿），第二阶段在非排尿事件中区分腹压与逼尿肌过度活动。采用独立数据集进行训练与外部验证，并通过交叉验证评估泛化性。性能指标包括准确率、F1-macro、AUC等。

Result: 第一阶段（排尿 vs 非排尿）性能：准确率84%（平衡准确率76%），F1-macro 0.74，AUC 0.85；第二阶段（腹压 vs 逼尿肌过度活动）性能：准确率90%（平衡准确率80%），F1-macro 0.80，AUC 0.87。特征重要性分析表明大多数特征对分类有显著贡献。

Conclusion: 该机器学习方案能够从单通道膀胱压力信号中准确自动识别泌尿事件，验证了单通道监测的可行性，为未来开发家用无线动态尿动力学监测系统奠定了技术基础。

Abstract: Objective: Conventional urodynamics (UDS) provide critical diagnostic information, but requires invasive dual catheterization and manual labeling of clinically important events. Wireless, catheter-free bladder function tests are becoming available for home use, but only provide vesical pressure (Pves). We developed a machine learning framework that was trained and externally validated on UDS data for automated urological event classification from single-channel (Pves) recordings. Methods: We analyzed 118 annotated UDS traces segmented into 0.8-second Pves intervals. Using the discrete wavelet transform, we extracted 55 statistical features per segment. Consecutive segments (233,338 segments; three classes) sharing the same class, abdominal (ABD), detrusor overactivity (DO), or voiding contraction (VOID), were grouped into events, and median feature aggregation was applied to derive event-level representations. Using an imbalanced dataset, we trained a two-stage multilayer perceptron (MLP): Stage 1 distinguished VOID vs non-VOID, and Stage 2 classified non-VOID into ABD and DO. The model was trained on two independent datasets and externally validated on a third independent dataset. Additional cross-dataset training-validation permutations were performed to assess generalizability. Performance was evaluated using accuracy, F1-macro, sensitivity, specificity, and area under the curve (AUC). Results: Stage 1 (VOID vs. non-VOID) achieved 84% accuracy (balanced accuracy 76%), F1-macro 0.74, and AUC 0.85, while Stage 2 (ABD vs. DO) reached 90% accuracy (balanced accuracy 80%), F1-macro 0.80, and AUC 0.87. Permutation feature importance indicated that most features contributed meaningfully. Conclusion: Our machine learning approach enables accurate automated detection of urological events from Pves, demonstrating feasibility for single-channel monitoring and future ambulatory applications.

### [3] [Low-Complexity Tensor Beamforming for RIS-Aided Multiuser Multistream MIMO Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22360)
*Bruno Sokal,André L. F. de Almeida,Martin Haardt*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文研究上行RIS辅助多用户多流MIMO系统的联合主动与被动波束成形，提出基于三阶复合信道张量的多流交替优化方法，通过低维张量投影联合设计接收合并器、块对角用户预编码器和RIS相位，仿真表明该方法在逼近多起点基准性能的同时降低计算复杂度并改善大规模RIS扩展性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统波束成形方法在RIS辅助大规模MIMO系统中面临计算复杂度高和扩展性差的挑战。由于接收合并器、用户预编码器和RIS相位的耦合设计可建模为三阶复合信道张量，利用张量的多线性结构特性有望实现高效联合优化，从而提升系统性能并降低计算开销。

Method: 提出多流张量交替优化算法。将联合设计问题建模为三阶复合信道张量形式，利用张量的多线性结构，通过低维张量投影交替更新接收合并器、块对角用户预编码器和RIS系数，采用块对角预编码结构保持性能的同时降低计算复杂度。

Result: 仿真结果表明，所提方法能够逼近多起点交替优化基准的性能，同时显著降低计算复杂度。在大规模RIS场景下，该方法展现出更好的扩展性，能够高效处理多用户多流传输需求。

Conclusion: 基于张量分解的交替优化方法有效解决了RIS辅助MIMO系统中联合主动和被动波束成形问题，在保证性能接近最优基准的同时，大幅降低计算复杂度并提高算法扩展性，为大规模RIS系统部署提供了可行方案。

Abstract: We address joint active and passive beamforming for uplink RIS-assisted multi-user multi-stream MIMO systems with joint detection. The coupled design of the receive combiner, block-diagonal user precoders, and RIS phase vector is formulated through a third-order composite channel tensor. Exploiting this multilinear structure, we propose a multi-stream tensor alternating optimization method that updates the combiner, user precoders, and RIS coefficients via low-dimensional tensor projections. Simulations show that the proposed method approaches a multi-start alternating-optimization benchmark while reducing computational complexity and improving large-RIS scaling.

### [4] [UAV-based Energy-Efficient Data Collection in Smart Grids with ISAC QoS Guarantees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22490)
*Yibin Xie,Jin Zhao,Indrakshi Dey,Nicola Marchetti*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文针对动态线路额定值(DLR)监测设备易受连接中断影响及无人机(UAV)能源受限的问题，提出了一种集成感知与通信(ISAC)的协同无人机数据收集框架。该框架通过联合优化无人机轨迹和收集调度，在满足DLR分钟级更新需求的同时显著降低能耗。提出的深度强化学习与半定松弛混合算法相比离线基准方案可降低34.6%的能耗，相比分离式感知通信方案可降低2.2%的能耗。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: DLR监测需要及时的数据来确定电力线路的实时容量，但监测设备易受连接中断影响导致数据缺失或延迟。虽然无人机可实现弹性数据收集，但其有限的机载能源难以支持长距离输电走廊的及时监测并应对飞行危险。因此，需要一种兼顾安全性和能源效率的协同数据收集方案来保障DLR的及时更新。

Method: 提出基于ISAC的协同无人机数据收集框架，将ISAC技术用于维持安全和协同数据收集所需的感知与通信质量。建立联合能量最小化问题，优化无人机轨迹和收集调度，并满足ISAC约束。为解决该问题，提出深度强化学习(DRL)与半定松弛(SDR)混合算法：DRL负责优化轨迹和调度策略，SDR用于处理非凸的ISAC约束。

Result: 仿真结果表明，所提方案相比离线基准方案可降低高达34.6%的能耗，相比分离式感知通信基线方案可降低约2.2%的能耗，同时满足DLR分钟级时间尺度要求。

Conclusion: 该研究验证了ISAC赋能的协同无人机框架在DLR监测中的有效性，通过DRL-SDR混合算法实现了显著的能耗节省，为长距离输电走廊的弹性监测提供了可行解决方案。

Abstract: Dynamic line rating (DLR) is a methodology that requires timely monitoring data to determine the real-time ampacity of power lines. However, DLR monitoring devices (MD) are vulnerable to connectivity disruptions, leading to missing or delayed data. Although unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV) can enable resilient data collection from MD, their limited onboard energy challenges timely monitoring over extended transmission corridors with flight hazards. This paper proposes a cooperative UAV-based data collection framework with integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) to support timely DLR updates. In this framework, ISAC is employed to maintain the sensing and communication quality required for safe and cooperative UAV data collection. Accordingly, a joint energy minimization problem is formulated over UAV trajectories and collection scheduling under ISAC constraints. To solve it, a hybrid algorithm combining deep reinforcement learning (DRL) and semidefinite relaxation (SDR) is proposed, where DRL optimizes the trajectory and collection scheduling, while SDR is used to handle the non-convex ISAC constraints. Simulation results show that the proposed scheme reduces energy consumption by up to 34.6% compared with offline benchmarks and by about 2.2% compared with the separated sensing-and-communication baseline, while satisfying the minute-level timescale requirement of DLR.
