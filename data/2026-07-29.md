### [1] [Beam Selection for Delay-Doppler Visibility in Multi-Target MIMO-OFDM Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25810)
*Amirhossein Azarbahram,Onel L. A. López*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文研究了多目标MIMO-OFDM传感中的泄漏感知波束选择问题，通过提出一种量化目标间干扰的可见度指标，并利用混合整数连续凸逼近方法优化波束选择与功率分配，有效解决了目标在时延-多普勒域相互泄漏导致的检测性能下降问题。

Motivation: 在多目标MIMO-OFDM感知场景中，受限于OFDM波形的模糊函数特性及空间角度耦合，不同目标在时延-多普勒（DD）域会产生相互泄漏，严重影响弱目标的检测。现有的感知设计缺乏对这种成对泄漏效应的充分考虑，因此需要研究一种泄漏感知的波束选择机制来优化目标可探测性。

Method: 1. 推导了一个显式的可见度度量指标，用于量化OFDM模糊核、接收角度耦合及发射码本增益引起的成对目标间干扰（泄漏）；2. 构建了最大-最小化（max-min）波束选择与功率分配优化问题；3. 采用基于二分法的混合整数连续凸逼近（MISCA）框架进行求解；4. 引入时间共享（time-sharing）模型作为全局最优基准进行对比验证。

Result: 数值仿真结果表明，所提出的泄漏感知设计在最差目标可见度指标上显著优于时间共享、全局泄漏抑制、基于照明的方案及随机波束选择基准，特别是在多目标空间分布密集（角距或DD距离小）的极端条件下，性能优势更为突出。

Conclusion: 本研究提出的基于成对泄漏感知的波束选择与功率分配方案，能够有效提升多目标MIMO-OFDM雷达在复杂场景下的感知性能，尤其是在目标角度或时延-多普勒空间相近时，该方法展现了比传统基准方法更优越的鲁棒性和可见度。

Abstract: This paper studies leakage-aware beam selection for multi-target multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO)-orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing (OFDM) sensing. We focus on ensuring that each hypothesized target remains detectable at its own delay-Doppler (DD) bin despite leakage from other targets. For this, we derive a visibility metric that separates the desired focused power from pairwise leakage caused by the finite-grid OFDM ambiguity kernel, receive angular coupling, and transmit codebook gains. Then, we formulate a max-min beam-selection and power-allocation problem in which the sensing signal over the fixed OFDM time-frequency grid is transmitted by a limited number of selected beams. The resulting problem is solved via a bisection-based mixed-integer successive convex approximation framework. A time-sharing formulation is also presented as a globally solvable benchmark. Numerical results show that the proposed pairwise leakage-aware design achieves higher worst-target visibility than the time-sharing, global leakage-suppression, illumination-based, and random beam-selection baselines, especially when targets are close in angle or DD.
