### [1] [RadioTrace: Transmitter-Aware Diffusion for Radio Map Estimation without Deployment-Time Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20909)
*Liu Yang,Qiang Li,Zhuo Cao,Weijie Xiong,Guomin Sun,Jingran Lin*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: RadioTrace 是一种无需部署阶段微调的无线电地图估计框架，通过将发射机定位显式集成到预训练扩散模型的去噪采样过程中，实现了对无线信号分布的高精度重建。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的无线电地图（RM）估计方法在处理复杂传播效应时效果不佳，或需要针对特定采样模式进行大规模重新训练，缺乏泛化能力；现有的基于先验的方法通常仅将生成模型视为简单的正则化器，且缺乏对发射机信息的显式整合。

Method: 提出了名为 RadioTrace 的框架，该框架：1) 将发射机（Tx）坐标估计整合进扩散模型的去噪循环中，实现发射机位置与信号分布的联合优化；2) 引入传播引导的 K-means 初始化方法，以避免优化陷入局部最优；3) 提供了关于发射机坐标细化过程的随机稳定性分析，保证了生成过程的可靠性。

Result: 实验结果表明，RadioTrace 在随机采样和受限区域采样场景下均能达到与当前最先进的基于学习的方法相当甚至更优的性能，证明了其在实际应用中的高效性与鲁棒性。

Conclusion: RadioTrace 通过将发射机定位与扩散模型采样过程深度耦合，无需部署阶段的微调即可实现高质量的无线电地图估计，展示了极强的通用性与稳健性。

Abstract: Radio map (RM) estimation aims to reconstruct the spatial distribution of wireless signal characteristics, such as received signal strength (RSS), from sparse measurements, a task that is critical for spectrum management, interference mitigation, and localization in modern wireless networks. Traditional approaches, including interpolation and deep learning, either struggle to capture complex propagation effects or require large-scale retraining for each new sampling pattern, which limits their generalization. More recently, prior-based methods have combined pre-trained generative models with measurements to reduce the need for deployment-time model fine-tuning, but they typically treat the prior as a simple regularizer and lack explicit transmitter-aware integration. In this paper, we propose RadioTrace, a novel RM estimation framework without deployment-time fine-tuning that tightly integrates sparse RSS measurements with a frozen pre-trained diffusion prior. RadioTrace incorporates transmitter (Tx) location estimation directly into the denoising loop, iteratively refining Tx coordinates based on reconstruction quality to guide the generative process. To further enhance robustness, we introduce a propagation-guided K-means initialization that mitigates poor local minima in the Tx update and provides a geometry-consistent starting point. Moreover, we provide a stochastic stability analysis for the Tx-coordinate refinement component, showing that the Tx update remains stable under perturbations induced by diffusion sampling and Tx-map relaxation. Extensive experiments demonstrate that RadioTrace achieves competitive performance with state-of-the-art learning-based methods under random sampling, and maintains strong reconstruction quality under restricted-area sampling, highlighting its adaptability, robustness, and practical relevance.

### [2] [Quantifying Event-Related (De)Synchronization Variability for Brain-Computer Interface: A Unified and Interpretable Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21119)
*Simon Kojima,Fabien Lotte*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种量化EEG信号在时、空、频维度变异性的可解释框架，发现降低变异性可提升BCI性能，并验证了不同分类模型对变异性的敏感度差异，为优化BCI系统鲁棒性提供了新途径。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 脑机接口（BCI）系统受限于用户内部及用户之间的EEG信号变异性。为了深入理解这种变异性，需要建立可解释的量化指标，以独立衡量BCI相关脑活动在时、空、频三个维度的变异程度。

Method: 研究通过提取EEG特征，利用适当的距离函数计算特征相对于质心的离散度，从而定量衡量时域、空域和频域的变异性。作者利用两个运动想象BCI数据集（N=133），通过用户内和跨用户分类实验，分析了这些变异性指标与BCI性能之间的相关性。

Result: 实验显示，变异性与BCI性能呈现负相关（-0.2到-0.4），即较低的变异性通常对应更高的分类准确率。此外，研究发现深度学习模型相比基于黎曼几何的分类器，对某些变异性表现出不同的鲁棒性。

Conclusion: 本文提出的变异性量化指标能有效揭示EEG信号特征，证明了降低变异性有助于提升脑机接口（BCI）性能，并揭示了不同分类模型对变异性的敏感度差异。该框架为构建鲁棒性更强的BCI系统提供了理论依据与评估工具。

Abstract: Objective: Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) enable the control of external devices by decoding user intentions from electroencephalography (EEG). However, substantial EEG variability within and between users remains a major challenge. To better understand this variability, we propose interpretable metrics that independently quantify temporal, spatial, and frequency variability in BCI related brain activity within and between users. Methods: We propose a framework to quantify variability by extracting EEG features and defining variability as their dispersion around their centroid using appropriate distance functions. Using two motor imagery BCI datasets (N = 133 users), we investigated the relationship between BCI performance and the variability metrics through within-user and cross-user classification experiments. Results: Negative correlations of -0.2 to -0.4 were observed across most conditions, suggesting that lower variability is associated with higher BCI performance. Moreover, the metrics revealed differences in robustness to variability between the deep learning and Riemannian-based classifiers, with the former showing weaker correlations. Conclusion: The results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed variability metrics and suggest that reducing variability may improve BCI performance while revealing differences in the sensitivity of classification models to different types of variability. Significance: The framework quantifies temporal, spatial, and frequency variability at multiple hierarchical levels (within-trial, between-trial, and between-trial-group), providing interpretable measures to better understand EEG variability and support more robust BCIs. It could also be used to characterize dataset variability, evaluate classifier sensitivity, incorporate variability into objective functions, and provide variability-based user feedback.

### [3] [Toward Generalizable Cognitive Impairment Detection with Speech-Based Multimodal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21496)
*Yingchao Huang,Xin Wang,Yuhan Su,Shanshan Yao*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 该研究提出了一种利用开源大语言模型整合语音和文本的多模态框架，在保护隐私的同时实现了92.4%的高精度认知障碍检测，在多个基准测试中达到了行业领先水平。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 认知障碍的早期准确诊断对及时干预至关重要。利用大语言模型在表征学习和泛化能力上的优势，结合语音信号中的语言学和声学特征，旨在开发一种更可靠、非侵入性的检测方法。

Method: 提出了一种基于开源大语言模型（LLM）的多模态认知障碍检测框架。该方法直接从语音信号中提取声学嵌入，并从自动转录的文本中提取文本嵌入，随后将两种模态特征拼接用于下游分类，整个过程无需访问原始敏感数据，从而保护了患者隐私。

Result: 在ADReSS20和ADReSSo21基准数据集上，该多模态框架的分类准确率达到了92.4%，表现优于单一模态基线，并展现了优秀的跨数据集泛化能力，建立了认知障碍检测的新基准。

Conclusion: 该研究表明，基于大语言模型的多模态框架融合语言学和声学数据，在认知障碍检测中具有卓越的性能，为临床提供了一种鲁棒、可扩展且非侵入性的高效筛查手段。

Abstract: Cognitive impairment (CI) is a growing public health concern. Early and accurate diagnosis is critical for enabling timely intervention and improving patient outcomes. Speech-based CI detection has emerged as a promising non-invasive approach, as speech signals encode both linguistic and acoustic markers associated with cognitive decline. Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) further strengthen the potential of speech-based assessment by enabling more expressive representation learning and improved generalization across diverse speakers, recording devices, and clinical environments. Moreover, multimodal learning by jointly modeling linguistic and acoustic features allows for a more comprehensive characterization of cognitive and behavioral changes related to CI, leading to more reliable detection. In this work, we propose a multimodal CI detection framework based on open-source LLMs that integrates speech audio and corresponding transcripts while preserving patient privacy. Acoustic embeddings are extracted directly from speech signals, while textual embeddings are generated from automatically transcribed speech. These modality-specific embeddings are then concatenated to create a combined feature vector and used for downstream classification, without requiring access to raw or sensitive patient data. The proposed approach is evaluated on the ADReSS20 and ADReSSo21 benchmark datasets. Experimental results show that the proposed multimodal framework achieves an CI classification accuracy of 92.4% and consistently outperforms single-modality baselines. Our work establishes a new state-of-the-art for CI identification, with the proposed method demonstrating superior cross-dataset generalization. This advance highlights the power of an LLM-based multimodal framework that fuses linguistic and acoustic data to enable robust, scalable, and non-invasive screening.
