### [3] [Sensing-Assisted Channel Estimation for Flexible-Antenna Systems: A Unified Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27626)

Motivation: 灵活天线系统虽为6G提供了硬件高效的架构，但海量候选天线位置导致信道状态信息（CSI）获取的导频开销呈指数级增长，成为制约系统性能的核心瓶颈，亟需低开销、高精度的信道估计解决方案。

It reduces the full CSI reconstruction problem to a consistent two-stage process: it first resolves the dominant DOAs from the uplink data symbols by exploiting the spatial geometry, requiring no dedicated sensing pilot, and then calibrates the associated path gains using a minimal number of calibration pilots. ### [4] [Semantics-Aware Hierarchical Token Communication: Clustering, Bit Mapping, and Power Allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27641)
