### [1] [Data Detection for Massive MIMO Systems with 1-Bit Quantized Dithered Linear Precoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05447)
*Amin Radbord,Italo Atzeni,Antti Tölli*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对使用1-bit DAC的大规模MIMO系统，本文提出了一种基于发射端抖动与符号相关最大似然（ML）检测的优化方案，有效缓解了量化失真并显著提升了数据恢复性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在全数字大规模MIMO系统中，高分辨率ADC/DAC的功耗极高。虽然1-bit DAC可以大幅降低功耗和硬件复杂度，但会引入严重的量化失真，亟需探索有效的信号处理手段来克服这一限制。

Method: 1. 在发射端采用预编码信号加抖动技术；
2. 提出基于软估计的数据检测方法，实现符号独立的抖动移除；
3. 提出一种新的符号相关线性化模型，用于直接从接收信号恢复数据；
4. 针对全分辨率和1-bit ADC分别推导最大似然（ML）检测方法，并开发了低复杂度的简化变体以应对流数增加带来的计算压力。

Result: 数值结果显示，所提基于ML的检测方法在符号误码率（SER）方面显著优于现有的基于同伦算法的二进制ML检测基准；同时，抖动功率的合理选取是提升性能的关键，低复杂度变体在大幅降低计算开销的同时，仍能保持接近最优的性能。

Conclusion: 该研究通过在发射端引入抖动信号，结合基于最大似然（ML）的符号相关线性化检测方法，显著提升了1-bit DAC在大规模MIMO系统中的传输性能。研究表明，合理配置抖动功率对于优化系统误码率至关重要，且所提出的低复杂度算法在保持高效的同时具备良好的实用性。

Abstract: The power consumption of the analog-to-digital converters (ADCs) and digital-to-analog converters (DACs) in fully digital massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) systems motivates the adoption of low-resolution architectures. In particular, 1-bit DACs reduce the power consumption and hardware complexity at the transmitter, but introduce severe transmit-side quantization distortion. In this paper, we investigate data detection for a point-to-point massive MIMO system with 1-bit DACs at the transmitter, where the linearly precoded signal is dithered prior to quantization, and either full-resolution or 1-bit ADCs at the receiver. Assuming that the dither vector applied at the transmitter is known at the receiver, we first develop softestimation-based data detection methods with symbol-independent dither removal for both full-resolution and 1-bit ADCs. We then introduce a new symbol-dependent linearization of the transmitted signal at the output of the 1-bit DACs and use it to derive maximum-likelihood (ML)-based data detection methods that directly recover the data symbol vector from the received signal. For full-resolution ADCs, this leads to an ML-based method with and without dither removal. For 1-bit ADCs, we develop an approximate ML-based method that exploits the derived statistics of the received signal without dither removal. We also propose low-complexity variants of the ML-based methods to mitigate the exponential complexity growth with the number of streams. Numerical results in terms of symbol error rate highlight the critical role of the dither power and demonstrate that the proposed ML-based methods (along with their low-complexity variants) achieve significant gains over a baseline based on binary ML detection via a homotopy algorithm.

### [2] [3D Spherical Fluid Antennas for Spatially Reconfigurable Communications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05589)
*Wenchi Cheng,Hongyun Jin,Zhuohui Yao*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种 3D 球形流体天线系统（3D SFAS），通过三维球空间内的区域切换与单元级重构，实现了阵列级空间灵活度，为解决 6G 复杂通信环境下的覆盖与自适应难题提供了创新方案。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的固定位置天线（FPA）受限于空间自由度，而现有的流体天线系统（FAS）研究多局限于二维平面，无法满足 6G 系统在复杂三维传播环境下的高性能与自适应通信需求。

Method: 提出了一种 3D 球形流体天线系统（3D SFAS）架构，通过在球体不同区域激活辐射单元实现阵列级空间重构，并结合区域内的单元级重构，实现了阵列拓扑、孔径大小及辐射特性的联合自适应控制。

Result: 数值仿真结果表明，3D SFAS 能够实现灵活的波束赋形、多区域并发传输及抗阻塞切换，通过三维空间重构显著优化了无线通信系统的整体性能。

Conclusion: 3D SFAS 通过从二维位置切换向全方位三维空间重构的跨越，为 6G 通信提供了物理层面的高度灵活性，显著提升了复杂无线环境下的通信性能，是未来空天地一体化网络及高移动性通信场景下的关键使能技术。

Abstract: As sixth-generation (6G) wireless systems evolve toward higher frequency bands, large-scale antenna arrays, and intelligent interaction with the wireless environment, conventional fixed-position antennas (FPAs) are increasingly constrained by limited spatial degrees of freedom and insufficient hardware-level adaptability. Fluid antenna systems (FAS) provide new physical-layer flexibility by dynamically reconfiguring antenna ports, geometries, and radiation characteristics. However, existing studies have mainly focused on one- or two-dimensional apertures, leaving the spatial reconfigurability required for complex three-dimensional (3D) propagation environments insufficiently exploited. In this article, we present a 3D spherical fluid antenna system (3D SFAS) architecture for flexible spatially reconfigurable communications. By activating radiating elements in different spherical regions, 3D SFAS realizes array-level spatial reconfiguration through flexible region switching. Within the selected regions, element-level reconfiguration further adjusts the effective aperture size, array topology, and radiation characteristics. This joint framework enables flexible beamforming, concurrent multi-region transmission, blockage-adaptive aperture switching, effective-aperture reconfiguration, and high-resolution 3D aperture control. We also discuss its potential applications in space-air-ground integrated networks, high-mobility communications, integrated sensing and communication systems, and emergency communications. Numerical results demonstrate the potential of 3D SFAS to improve wireless communication performance through flexible spatial reconfiguration. Overall, 3D SFAS extends FAS design beyond 2D position switching toward comprehensive 3D spatial reconfigurability.

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于变分贝叶斯推理的宽带太赫兹通信联合信道估计与波束赋形框架，通过结合Bussgang分解和真时间延迟（TTD）架构，有效解决了低分辨率ADC失真、宽带波束偏移及复杂硬件约束下的通信难题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 太赫兹（THz）频段在硬件受限、频率相关传播效应以及部分连接架构下的信道估计与波束赋形面临严峻挑战，传统的窄带方法难以解决宽带波束偏移（beam-squint）及低精度量化带来的非线性失真问题。

Method: 1. 建立包含根升余弦（RRC）脉冲整形的双宽带信道模型；2. 利用Bussgang分解处理低分辨率ADC带来的非线性失真；3. 提出基于变分贝叶斯推理的联合信道估计与波束赋形算法，支持网格上（on-grid）与网格下（off-grid）角度估计；4. 引入基于真时间延迟（TTD）的混合收发架构以补偿宽带波束偏移效应。

Result: 推导了多用户贝叶斯克拉美-罗下界（BCRLB）作为性能基准，仿真结果显示该框架能有效捕捉空间稀疏性，在复杂宽带太赫兹环境下表现出比传统方法更高的估计精度和波束对准性能。

Conclusion: 该研究证明了基于变分贝叶斯推理的联合估计与波束赋形框架在处理太赫兹通信中的非理想因素（如低精度ADC、硬件约束和波束偏移）方面具有显著的有效性和鲁棒性，为未来宽带太赫兹系统的实际部署提供了理论与技术支撑。

Abstract: This work conceives a unified channel estimation and beamforming framework, formulated within the principles of variational Bayesian inference. Recognizing the limitations imposed by hardware constraints, frequency-dependent propagation effects, and the structural restrictions of partially connected architectures in the Terahertz (THz) band, we formulate a dual-wideband channel model incorporating root raised cosine (RRC) pulse shape to account its band-limited nature. To further address the nonlinear distortions introduced by low-resolution ADCs, Bussgang decomposition is employed, enabling a tractable linearized inference process. Unlike conventional techniques, the proposed method accommodates both on-grid and off-grid angular domains, capturing spatial sparsity with improved resolution and robustness. The multi-user (MU) Bayesian Cramér-Rao lower bound is also derived to benchmark the performance of the proposed estimator. Moreover, the framework incorporates a true time delay (TTD)-based hybrid transceiver design that inherently compensates for the beam-squint effect; a frequency-dependent angular deviation that arises due to the fixedphase nature of the conventional beamformer in wideband systems, thereby ensuring accurate directional alignment across all subcarriers. Extensive simulation results validate the effectiveness of the proposed variational Bayesian inference-based estimator and the TTD-enabled beamforming architecture, highlighting their robustness and performance gains under practical wideband THz system.

Abstract: 6G networks will introduce unprecedented complexity, which calls for a paradigm shift in network optimization and management. ### [5] [LatentWave: JEPA Pretraining for Wireless Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06373)
*Ahmed Mohamed,Ahmed Aboulfotouh,Hatem Abou-Zeid*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: LatentWave 是一种新型无线基础模型，通过在潜在空间进行预测而非重构原始信号，显著增强了跨任务的可迁移性，并能灵活处理异构的天线配置。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的无线基础模型多依赖于掩码输入重构（Masked Input Reconstruction），这容易使模型特征偏向于低层信号细节，从而限制了模型在不同下游任务间的泛化能力。因此，旨在开发一种更能捕捉高层语义、具备更强跨任务迁移能力的无线基础模型。

Method: 提出了 LatentWave 模型，采用联合嵌入预测架构（JEPA）代替传统的掩码输入重构，通过在潜在空间预测被掩码的区域来学习特征。同时，引入了每通道补丁嵌入（per-channel patch embeddings）和训练期间的随机通道采样技术，以适应异构无线天线配置。

Result: 在四个下游任务（RF信号分类、5G NR定位、波束预测、视距/非视距分类）上的实验表明，LatentWave 优于基于掩码建模的基准模型（WavesFM）。研究还发现，频率掩码（frequency masking）对定位和波束预测等信道相关任务效果更好，而区域掩码（region masking）则更能保留信号分类任务的辨别力。

Conclusion: LatentWave 证明了采用联合嵌入预测架构（JEPA）并在潜在空间中进行预测，能有效提升无线基础模型在多任务场景下的泛化能力与可迁移性。此外，掩码几何结构的策略性选择可作为一种归纳偏置，针对不同类型的无线任务进行优化。

Abstract: Wireless foundation models have emerged as a promising alternative to building separate models for each wireless task. However, existing approaches rely on masked input reconstruction, which can bias representations toward low-level signal details. In this paper, we propose LatentWave, a wireless foundation model pretrained using a Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) on diverse wireless spectrograms and channel state information (CSI). By predicting masked regions in latent space, LatentWave learns representations that are more transferable out of the box across diverse downstream tasks. The proposed architecture employs per-channel patch embeddings with stochastic channel sampling during pretraining, allowing it to process variable antenna counts and improving usability across heterogeneous wireless configurations. We evaluate LatentWave on four downstream tasks: RF signal classification, 5G NR positioning, beam prediction, and LoS/NLoS classification, comparing against a masked-modeling baseline (WavesFM) pretrained on the same data. Additionally, we show that the masking geometry introduces a task-dependent inductive bias: frequency masking strongly favors channel-related tasks such as positioning and beam prediction, while region masking better preserves discriminability for signal classification.
