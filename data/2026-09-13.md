### [1] [From Semantic to Token Communication: The Next Paradigm for Large-Model-Driven 6G Intelligent Connectivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10714)
*Yu Ma,Zhen Gao,Li Qiao,Xiaoyuan Zhang,Mahdi Boloursaz Mashhadi,Yin Xu,Wenjun Xu,Xiaodong Xu,Kaibin Huang,Jiangzhou Wang,Rahim Tafazolli,Sheng Chen,Tony Q. S. Quek,Ping Zhang*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了“Token通信（TokenCom）”，将大模型的Token作为语义通信的通用度量单位和通信抽象，以解决当前语义通信碎片化的问题，为6G AI原生系统提供了一套可扩展、统一的通信范式。

Motivation: 当前大模型驱动的语义通信存在模态与任务割裂的碎片化问题，缺乏类似于信息论中“比特（bit）”的通用语义度量单元，制约了语义通信的互操作性、理论统一与系统扩展性。

Method: 本文首先综述了三种主流的LM驱动语义通信方向（信源语义编码、物理层信道语义、协作边缘智能）；其次，论证了以Token作为通用语义表示单元的必然性，提出将Token抽象作为网络传输层与LM处理层之间的接口，并探讨了针对Token的资源分配、差错控制和传输技术。

Result: 文章提出了TokenCom的概念框架，论证了其在处理多模态统一表示及大模型分布式推理中的优势。通过对Token级粒度的重要性分配与资源调度，证明了该范式能够有效支持大模型服务、具身智能及智能体协同等新兴通信需求。

Conclusion: 本文确立了“Token通信”（TokenCom）作为6G语义通信的核心范式。通过将大模型（LM）的Token作为统一的通信抽象，解决了传统语义通信模态割裂、缺乏通用表示单元的问题，为实现AI原生、可扩展的语义通信系统提供了理论基础与实践路径。

Abstract: The ambitious requirements of sixth-generation (6G) networks are driving communication systems from reliable bit delivery toward meaning-aware and task-oriented connectivity. Large models (LMs), with strong multimodal understanding and generation capabilities, have accelerated this shift and made semantic communication (SemCom) increasingly practical. Yet current LM-driven SemCom remains fragmented: semantic representations are typically tied to specific modalities, models, or tasks. While the bit provides a universal unit for digital transport, there is still no analogous unit for representing and processing semantics, which limits interoperability, theoretical unification, and scalable system design. We argue that tokens provide a natural candidate for this missing abstraction. Two trends support this: unified multimodal LMs now encode text, images, audio, video, and robot actions in one token space, while distributed LM inference already generates substantial token-level traffic through expert routing, cache transfer, and speculative decoding. Token communication (TokenCom) emerges by unifying these trends, using the LM's native processing unit as a communication abstraction above the bit level and enabling importance assignment, error handling, and resource allocation directly at token granularity. This survey traces the evolution from LM-driven SemCom to TokenCom. We review three major directions of LM-driven SemCom: source-centric semantic coding, channel semantics for physical-layer tasks, and collaborative edge-device intelligence. We then examine the token abstraction, the transmission techniques it requires, and two emerging paradigms, namely TokenCom for LM services and for embodied and agentic intelligence. Finally, we identify open challenges toward unified, scalable, and AI-native 6G communication systems.

### [2] [Optimizing GEDI Simulator Configuration for European Temperate Forests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11440)
*Selim Behloul,Nikola Besic,Steven Hancock,Cedric Vega,Sylvie Durrieu,Jean-Pierre Renaud,Ibrahim Fayad,Philippe Ciais*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本研究在法国森林中对GEDI波形模拟器进行了验证与改进，通过优化L2A算法（推荐a3）和参数设置，显著降低了模拟误差，并证明了将叶落期及低灵敏度数据纳入分析可有效提升森林生物量估算的能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 准确估算地上生物量密度对于量化森林碳储量至关重要。NASA的GEDI任务虽然提供了宝贵的冠层结构数据，但由于其采样稀疏，需要依靠模拟器在实地调查地点对生物量模型进行校准。目前广泛使用的模拟器虽已存在，但此前尚未在欧洲温带森林中进行过验证，亟需通过实证研究评估其性能及潜在的改进空间。

Method: 研究团队利用法国国家机载激光雷达（ALS）点云数据，对约9,500对真实的GEDI波形与模拟GEDI相对高度（RH）剖面进行了对比分析。通过将模拟和真实RH指标参考至统一的ALS衍生地面高程，分离出波形建模差异和地面检测偏差两个误差来源，并评估了不同的GEDI L2A处理算法及其参数配置（如强度加权返回）的性能。

Result: 研究发现，在基准配置下，叶茂期和叶落期的平均绝对偏差分别为0.69米和1.28米。通过改用基于强度的返回加权并选择a3算法，可以将偏差分别降低至0.44米和0.40米。a3算法将地面检测偏差从默认的-0.88米显著降低至-0.03米。此外，研究证实叶落期采集和低灵敏度射击的数据同样可靠，可被纳入校准和推断数据集，从而显著提升数据利用效率。

Conclusion: 该研究验证了Hancock等人（2019）提出的GEDI波形模拟器在欧洲温带森林中的适用性，并证明通过优化处理算法和参数设置，可以显著降低模拟误差。研究强调了选择正确的L2A算法（如a3）对于减少地面检测偏差的关键作用，并指出扩大数据集（包括叶落期数据和低灵敏度射击）对于提高森林生物量估算的准确性具有重要潜力。

Abstract: Accurate estimation of aboveground biomass density is essential for quantifying forest carbon stocks. NASA's GEDI mission provides valuable canopy structure data, but its sparse sampling necessitates the use of simulators to calibrate biomass models at field inventory locations. The widely used simulator of Hancock et al. (2019) emulates GEDI waveforms from airborne LiDAR point clouds, yet it has never been validated over European temperate forests. Here, we compare approximately 9,500 pairs of observed and simulated GEDI relative height (RH) profiles across French forests using the national airborne LiDAR program as input. We separate two sources of error: waveform modeling differences, assessed by referencing both simulated and real RH metrics to a common ALS-derived ground elevation, and ground detection bias, evaluated by comparing each GEDI L2A processing algorithm against the ALS reference. Under the baseline configuration, the mean absolute bias across the full RH profile reaches 0.69 m in leaf-on and 1.28 m in leaf-off conditions. Switching to intensity-based return weighting and selecting the a3 L2A algorithm reduces these biases to 0.44 m and 0.40 m respectively. The a3 algorithm also achieves near-unbiased ground detection (-0.03 m versus -0.88 m for the default), directly reducing a previously overlooked source of error. We also show that leaf-off acquisitions and low-sensitivity shots, both typically excluded from standard biomass products, are simulated as reliably as their counterparts, substantially expanding the potential calibration and inference datasets.
