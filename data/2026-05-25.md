### [1] [JointHRRP-Net: A Statistically Constrained Decoupling Network for Joint Target and Jamming Recognition in Composite Jamming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22857)
*Yunfei Zhao,Mei Liu,Shuowei Liu,Xunzhang Gao,Yujie Zhou*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文针对复合干扰环境下基于高分辨距离像(HRRP)的雷达自动目标识别性能严重退化问题，提出JointHRRP-Net统一框架。该框架通过统计约束解耦模块分离目标与干扰特征，利用多尺度时序编码建模散射结构，并采用双专家决策模块实现联合识别。实验表明其在多种干噪比/信噪比条件下均优于基线方法，且支持未知目标拒绝。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 复合干扰环境中的主动干扰在脉冲压缩后与目标回波耦合，导致目标散射峰难以区分、特征可分性下降，严重影响了HRRP-based雷达自动目标识别性能。现有方法难以有效解决目标与干扰特征之间的纠缠问题。

Method: 提出JointHRRP-Net框架：(1)统计约束解耦模块，通过相关性引导的统计约束从混合距离像中生成目标主导和干扰主导的潜在分支，抑制跨分支冗余信息；(2)多尺度时序编码模块，建模局部散射结构和长程距离单元依赖关系；(3)双专家决策模块，分别执行单标签目标分类和多标签干扰分类。

Result: 在多样化的干噪比(SJR)和信噪比(SNR)条件下，JointHRRP-Net在目标识别和复合干扰识别方面均显著优于代表性基线方法。开放集评估表明，学习到的目标表示对未知目标拒绝仍保持高判别性。

Conclusion: JointHRRP-Net能有效解决复合干扰场景下的目标-干扰特征纠缠问题，所提框架在目标识别、干扰识别及未知目标拒绝任务中均表现出显著的有效性和鲁棒性，为复杂电磁环境下的雷达自动目标识别提供了新思路。

Abstract: High-resolution range profile (HRRP)-based radar automatic target recognition suffers from severe performance degradation in composite jamming environments. Active jamming introduces suppression- and deception-related components into the received range profile. After pulse compression, these components are coupled with target echoes in the HRRP domain, making target-related scattering peaks difficult to distinguish and weakening feature separability. To address this problem, this paper proposes JointHRRP-Net, a unified framework for joint target-jamming recognition. A statistically constrained decoupling module is first developed to generate target-dominant and jamming-dominant latent branches from the mixed HRRP representation. Correlation-guided statistical constraints are imposed to suppress redundant cross-branch information and alleviate target-jamming feature entanglement. A multi-scale temporal encoding module is then designed to model local scattering structures and long-range range-cell dependencies, followed by a dual-expert decision module for single-label target classification and multi-label jamming classification. Experiments under diverse signal-to-jamming ratio (SJR) and signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) levels demonstrate that JointHRRP-Net outperforms representative baseline methods in both target recognition and composite jamming recognition. Open-set evaluation further shows that the learned target representation remains discriminative for unknown-target rejection. These results demonstrate the effectiveness and robustness of JointHRRP-Net in composite jamming scenarios.

### [2] [Classification of IED-free EEG Responses for Assisted Epilepsy Diagnosis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22858)
*Giacomo Zanardini,Ryan Moesman,Paul van der Kleij,Robert van den Berg,Justin Dauwels*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本研究针对IEDs阴性癫痫诊断难题，提出基于时域、频域、小波域和连接域特征的多域堆叠集成机器学习流程，对IPS和HV刺激下的EEG进行分类。在TUH和EMC数据集上的留一被试出验证显示，该方法在静息态和IPS脑电图上分别达到97.8%/93.1%和94.1%/86.8%的AUC/BAC，证实刺激诱发活动提供鉴别信息且集成提升鲁棒性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当常规脑电图缺乏间期癫痫样放电时，癫痫诊断困难。间歇性光刺激和过度换气可提高检出率，但判读主观。本研究旨在开发客观、可重复的自动化分析方法。

Method: 提出可重复的机器学习流程：从时域、频域、小波域和连接域提取特征，采用堆叠集成融合互补特征集。使用留一被试出交叉验证，在TUH癫痫数据库和Erasmus MC临床队列上评估，特别分析IEDs阴性样本。

Result: TUH数据集：IEDs阴性静息态EEG达97.8% AUC/93.1% BAC；IEDs阴性IPS达94.1% AUC/86.8% BAC。EMC队列：IPS区分度最佳（79.4% AUC/73.9% BAC）；HV性能通过被试反应性分层提升。

Conclusion: 刺激诱发活动（尤其IPS）包含有意义鉴别信息，可用于IEDs阴性癫痫分类。多域集成策略提高模型鲁棒性，为临床提供客观诊断工具。

Abstract: Diagnosing epilepsy is challenging when routine EEGs lack interictal epileptiform discharges (IEDs). Intermittent photic stimulation (IPS) and hyperventilation (HV) can increase diagnostic yield, but their interpretation is subjective. We propose a reproducible pipeline that classifies EEG recordings acquired during stimulation procedures, using machine-learning features spanning temporal, spectral, wavelet, and connectivity domains, and a stacked ensemble to combine complementary feature sets. Performance is evaluated with leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) cross-validation on the TUH Epilepsy Corpus and a clinical Erasmus MC (EMC) cohort, including IED-free analyses on TUH. On TUH, ensembles achieve up to 97.8\% AUC / 93.1\% BAC on IED-free resting-state EEG and 94.1\% AUC / 86.8\% BAC on IED-free IPS. On EMC, IPS provides the strongest discrimination (79.4\% AUC / 73.9\% BAC), while HV performance benefits from stratifying subjects by responsiveness. These results indicate that stimulation-evoked activity, particularly IPS, contains meaningful discriminative information for IED-free epilepsy classification and that multi-domain ensembling improves robustness.

Abstract: Direct water-to-air (W2A) optical communications experience strong beam refraction at the dynamic sea surface. By accounting for link interruptions due to total internal reflection (TIR) and receiver field-of-view limitations, we derive closed-form expressions for the channel distribution and link outage probability. Our analysis reveals a fundamental TIR-induced outage floor limiting link reliability and providing insight for robust W2A system design.

### [4] [Self-Calibration DOA Estimation for Movable Antenna Systems with Antenna Position Errors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23140)
*Chengzhi Ye,Ruoyu Zhang,Wen Wu,Byonghyo Shim*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 该论文针对存在未知天线位置误差的可移动天线系统，提出了一种基于交替优化的自校准DOA估计算法。通过利用导向矢量与噪声子空间的正交性，将问题转化为优化问题，迭代求解天线位置误差和波达方向。仿真结果表明该方法优于现有方案。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 可移动天线系统中的未知天线位置误差会严重影响DOA估计性能，传统方法难以实现鲁棒性估计。需要开发一种能够在位置误差存在的情况下仍保持高精度的自校准算法。

Method: 采用交替优化框架，分两个阶段迭代求解：第一阶段固定天线位置误差，使用MUSIC算法估计DOA；第二阶段固定DOA，通过拉格朗日乘子技术推导出天线位置误差的闭式解。反复迭代直至收敛。

Result: 仿真结果验证了所提自校准算法在可移动天线系统中具有更优越的DOA估计性能，相较于现有方法表现出明显的精度优势。

Conclusion: 该交替优化自校准算法有效解决了可移动天线系统中天线位置误差带来的DOA估计问题，为鲁棒无线感知提供了可行方案。

Abstract: In this letter, we investigate the direction-of-arrival (DOA) estimation problem for wireless sensing with movable antenna (MA) systems in the presence of unknown antenna position errors (APE). To achieve robust wireless sensing, we transform the DOA estimation problem with APE into an optimization problem via the orthogonality between the steering vector and the noise subspace. Then we propose an alternating optimization (AO)-based self-calibration estimation, which consists of two stages and iteratively estimates the APE and DOA. Specifically, in the first stage, by fixing the APE, the problem reduces to the classical DOA estimation problem, which is solved using the multiple signal classification (MUSIC) algorithm. In the second stage, we fix the DOA to estimate the APE. By applying the Lagrange multiplier technique to the subproblem, we obtain a closed-form expression for the APE estimation. Simulation results demonstrate the superior DOA estimation performance of the proposed self-calibration algorithm for MA systems compared to the existing approaches.

### [5] [Communication Security and Sensing Privacy in FMCW-Based ISAC Through Signal Modulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23429)
*Murat Temiz,Christos Masouros*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 该论文提出一种基于雷达的集成感知与通信(ISAC)系统安全信号设计方案。通过在FMCW雷达chirp上联合采用索引调制和相位编码，在保障高数据吞吐量的同时，实现物理层安全通信与感知隐私增强，有效对抗未授权被动感知窃听。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ISAC系统中通信信号同时用于感知，导致感知隐私泄露风险。传统加密无法阻止被动感知窃听者(S-Eve)从信号中提取目标信息，现有方案难以兼顾通信安全与感知隐私，亟需一种能在物理层同时保护两者的创新方法。

Method: 采用雷达中心框架，基于FMCW雷达chirp设计：1)索引调制(IM)在激活模式上嵌入信息，提供外层数据安全性；2)相位编码(PC)刻意扰动信号模糊函数，使窃听者难以准确估计目标速度与距离；3)提出完整的发射端与接收端架构，支持合法感知操作与信号解调。

Result: 仿真验证显示，该方案在维持高数据吞吐量的同时，对未授权感知窃听者造成显著影响：目标速度估计基本失效，距离估计精度严重下降，而合法用户通信安全性和感知性能不受影响。

Conclusion: 该雷达中心信号设计为ISAC系统提供了有效的物理层安全解决方案，通过IM与PC的联合优化，实现了通信安全与感知隐私的协同增强，为未来智能系统的安全部署提供了新思路。

Abstract: This study proposes a novel radar-centric signaling design and architecture for secure integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) systems. The proposed framework is designed to provide robust physical layer security for data transmission while simultaneously enhancing sensing privacy. It employs index modulation and phase coding over frequency-modulated continuous-wave radar (FMCW) chirps, where index modulation (IM) provides an outer layer of data security, and we explicitly design the phase coding (PC) to perturb the resulting signal's ambiguity function (AF) to enhance sensing privacy. This design reduces the risk of unauthorized surveillance by rendering target velocity estimation practically infeasible for unauthorized passive sensing hardware (i.e., a sensing eavesdropper, S-Eve) and significantly impairing its range estimation capabilities. Furthermore, this study also presents the transmitter and receiver architectures required for effective modulation and demodulation of the proposed ISAC signaling and for performing sensing at the legitimate sensing hardware. Simulation results show that the proposed approach achieves high data throughput while enhancing communication security and sensing privacy.
