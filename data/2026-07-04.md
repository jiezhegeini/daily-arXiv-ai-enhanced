### [1] [Generalizable framework of eating episode detection on free-living wrist-worn wearable data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01376)
*Chunzhuo Wang,Emma De Schuyteneer,Huaidong Du,Aiden Doherty,Elske Vrieze,Bart Vanrumste*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对现有进食检测技术泛化性差和对陀螺仪依赖度高的问题，研究提出了一种旋转不变的通用检测框架，并通过跨数据集验证证明了其在多种实际场景下的鲁棒性，同时利用创新模块提升了仅加速度计配置下的性能。

Motivation: 现有进食行为检测方法多依赖于单一数据集的内部验证，对传感器方向、佩戴习惯及数据异构性适应性差；此外，许多高性能模型依赖陀螺仪，但在资源受限的实际部署中，仅依靠加速度计往往导致性能下降。因此，研究旨在开发一种具备高度泛化能力且能克服传感器局限性的进食检测框架。

Method: 提出了一种通用的旋转不变进食片段检测框架，引入了acc2gyro模块（将加速度信号映射为陀螺仪特征）以优化仅有加速度计场景下的性能，并采用跨数据集的外部验证方法，在三个异构数据集（CAD、Capture-24、Physio-ED）上进行了广泛测试。

Result: 该框架在三个外部验证数据集上表现稳健，F1分数分别为0.751（CAD）、0.592（Capture-24）和0.793（Physio-ED）。在CAD数据集上的表现超过了以往仅使用内部验证的最先进方法，且acc2gyro模块有效提升了仅使用加速度计场景下的检测精度。

Conclusion: 该研究证明了旋转不变性可穿戴传感框架在评估进食行为方面的潜力，不仅提高了泛化能力，还通过acc2gyro模块解决了仅使用加速度计时的性能瓶颈，为饮食失调及慢性病患者的临床应用提供了可扩展的方案。

Abstract: Accurate assessment of eating behavior is essential for understanding and managing conditions such as eating disorders, obesity, and diabetes. Wearable-based food intake detection has shown considerable promise; however, most existing approaches are trained and evaluated using internal validation on a single dataset with fixed sensor orientation and known wearing hand, which limits their generalizability to real-world settings. Furthermore, many existing approaches rely on both accelerometer (acc) and gyroscope (gyro) signals to achieve strong performance. However, gyro measurements may be unavailable in some real-world deployments due to battery constraints, and performance often degrades when only acc data are used. We propose a generalizable framework for orientation-invariant eating episode detection, with an acc2gyro module to improve performance in acc-only settings. The framework is trained using fine-grained wrist-worn datasets and externally validated across three heterogeneous datasets: the Clemson All-Day (CAD) and Capture-24 datasets, as well as Physio-ED, a dataset collected from individuals with eating disorders. Across external evaluations, the proposed framework demonstrates robust performance despite substantial variations in sensor modality, wearing hand, participant population, and annotation protocols. Specifically, the framework achieved F1-scores of 0.751, 0.592, and 0.793 on CAD, Capture-24, and Physio-ED, respectively, with CAD performance exceeding recent state-of-the-art methods evaluated using internal validation only. This study provides the first external validation of eating episode detection in an eating disorder population. Additionally, the acc2gyro module improves the performance in acc-only settings. These findings demonstrate the potential of orientation-invariant wearable sensing for scalable and clinically applicable assessment of eating behavior.

### [2] [Scene-Conditioned PINN-GNN for Multipath RF Maps: Cross-Scene Generation and In-Scene Completion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01777)
*Lizhou Liu,Xiaohui Chen,Zihan Tang,Mengyao Ma,Wenyi Zhang*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种融合PINN（物理一致性）与GNN（空间一致性）的射频地图构建框架，在稀疏观测下实现了优于现有主流方法的高精度多径参数重构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的射频地图构建方法在稀疏观测条件下难以准确重构多径传播特性，且缺乏对物理传播规律的有效利用。因此，需要一种能够结合物理约束与空间相关性，实现跨场景泛化和场景内补全的高保真射频地图构建框架。

Method: 本文提出了一个统一的射频地图构建框架，主要包含两个核心组件：1. 物理信息神经网络（PINN），用于将接收机位置映射到多径参数（增益、到达时间和角度），并嵌入电磁传播物理约束；2. 图神经网络（GNN），通过建模接收机间的邻域相关性来强化空间一致性。此外，本文提出了一种峰值加权动态时间规整（Peak-weighted DTW）度量标准，用于评估多径重构质量。

Result: 实验结果表明，该方法在地图级和多径级指标上均优于基于图像、扩散模型和插值的基准模型，即使在稀疏观测下也能实现鲁棒的泛化和高保真的射频地图重构。

Conclusion: 本文提出了一种结合物理信息神经网络（PINN）和图神经网络（GNN）的射频地图构建框架，该方法通过物理约束保证了多径参数的准确性，通过图结构保证了空间一致性，为复杂电磁环境下的无线信道建模提供了鲁棒且高效的解决方案。

Abstract: Radio frequency (RF) maps provide a compact representation of multipath propagation characteristics and are fundamental to channel modeling, coverage analysis, and environment-aware wireless optimization. This paper proposes a unified RF map construction framework based on a physics-informed neural network (PINN) and a graph neural network (GNN), supporting both cross-scene generation and in-scene completion with 2D and 2.5D environmental representations. The PINN embeds electromagnetic propagation constraints to establish a physically consistent mapping from receiver locations to multipath parameters, including path gain, time of arrival, and angles, while the GNN enforces spatial consistency by modeling correlations among neighboring receivers. To comprehensively evaluate multipath reconstruction quality, we propose a peak-weighted dynamic time warping metric that jointly accounts for amplitude errors and peak delay misalignment in channel impulse responses. Extensive experiments demonstrate that the proposed method consistently outperforms image-based, diffusion-based, and interpolation baselines across both map-level and multipath-level metrics, achieving robust generalization and high-fidelity RF map construction under sparse observations.
