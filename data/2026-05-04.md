### [1] [TimeRFT: Stimulating Generalizable Time Series Forecasting for TSFMs via Reinforcement Finetuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00015)
*Siyang Li,Yize Chen,Zijie Zhu,Yuxin Pan,Yan Guo,Ming Huang,Hui Xiong*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 该研究针对时间序列基础模型（TSFM）在下游任务适应中面临的时序分布漂移与数据效率瓶颈，提出一种新型强化微调范式TimeRFT。通过设计预测质量驱动的时序奖励机制与难度感知的数据选择策略，有效缓解监督微调导致的过拟合问题，显著提升模型在多样化数据场景下的预测精度与泛化鲁棒性。

Motivation: 尽管时间序列基础模型通过大规模统一预训练提升了泛化性与数据效率，但其在特定下游预测任务中的适应仍存在明显短板：首先，时间序列数据的非平稳性与不确定性造成历史训练与未来测试数据间的时序分布漂移，而现有监督微调（SFT）方法易过拟合，损害泛化能力；其次，不同预测任务的数据可用性差异显著，要求模型在多种数据机制下均保持强泛化性能。

Method: 提出时间序列强化微调（TimeRFT）框架，包含两个核心组件：其一，基于预测质量的时序奖励机制，对每个预测步长的贡献进行多维度评估以指导模型优化；其二，基于预测难度的数据选择策略，筛选具有可泛化预测模式与高信息量的训练样本，从数据层面提升微调质量。

Result: 在多个真实世界预测任务与不同数据机制下的综合实验表明，TimeRFT相较SFT方法能持续提升预测准确率，并在面对未预见分布漂移时展现出更强的泛化鲁棒性。

Conclusion: TimeRFT为时间序列基础模型的下游适应提供了强化学习新思路，通过奖励机制与数据选择的协同优化，有效应对了时序分布漂移挑战，在预测性能与泛化能力上显著超越传统监督微调基线。

Abstract: Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) advance generalization and data efficiency in time series forecasting by unified large-scale pretraining. But TSFMs remain lacking when adapting to specific downstream forecasting tasks for two reasons. First, the non-stationary and uncertain nature of time series data lead to inevitable temporal distribution shifts between historical training and future testing data, while current Supervised FineTuning (SFT)-based methods are prone to overfitting and may degrade generalization. Second, training data availability varies across forecasting tasks, requiring TSFMs to generalize well under diverse data regimes. To address these challenges, we introduce the Time series Reinforcement Finetuning (TimeRFT) paradigm for TSFM downstream adaptation, which consists of two task-specific training recipes: i) A forecasting quality-based temporal reward mechanism that conducts a multi-faceted evaluation of the contribution of each prediction step to overall forecasting accuracy. ii) A forecasting difficulty-based data selection strategy to identify time series samples with generalizable predictive patterns and informative training signals. Extensive experiments demonstrate TimeRFT can consistently outperform SFT-based adaptation methods across various real-world forecasting tasks and training data regimes, enhancing prediction accuracy and generalization against unforeseen distribution shifts.

### [2] [Multi-Objective Adaptive Beamforming Using Partial Knowledge of Dynamic Dielectric Media for Non-Invasive Microwave Hyperthermia](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00215)
*Ahona Bhattacharyya,Tessa Haldes,Jeffrey A. Nanzer,Susan C. Hagness*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对非侵入式微波热疗中因介电特性时变导致的能量沉积失准问题，本研究提出基于线性约束最小功率(LCMP)算法的多目标自适应波束形成策略。通过在2.5GHz载频下实现靶向聚焦加热（~45°C）并抑制正常组织温升，结合时域有限差分仿真，在均质/非均质介质及真实乳腺模型中验证算法在介电参数不确定条件下的有效性，为实时自适应热疗系统提供设计依据。

Motivation: 传统微波热疗波束形成器依赖静态权值，但加热过程会引起组织介电特性变化，导致性能退化。现有患者特异性介电图谱获取方法（MRI/微波层析成像）耗时且难以实时应用，限制了临床响应速度。因此，亟需开发能够在介电特性动态变化且先验知识有限条件下，仍能维持靶向精度并保护健康组织的自适应波束形成技术。

Result: 研究系统评估了LCMP算法在三种场景下的性能：简单均质模型验证基础性能；非均质模型评估介电不均匀性影响；MRI衍生乳腺体模验证临床适用性。通过热图分析，验证了算法在介电参数不确定条件下实现选择性加热与正常组织保护的能力。

Conclusion: 本工作为开发实时自适应微波热疗系统提供了理论框架和技术验证，证明LCMP算法在应对介电特性动态变化方面的潜力。研究成果将指导未来无需实时介电成像即可实现精准靶向加热的临床设备设计，提升热疗的安全性和有效性。

Abstract: We investigate multi-objective adaptive beamformer design strategies for non-invasive microwave hyperthermia. Our focus is to address the challenges of maintaining focused power deposition in desired locations while reducing unwanted heating elsewhere under conditions of changing dielectric properties. The process of heating the media causes changes in the dielectric properties of the media, which can degrade the effectiveness beamformers with static weights. Typical hyperthermic beamformer designs calculate antenna beamforming weights using patient-specific high resolution dielectric maps obtained by MRI or microwave tomography, however this process is time consuming and difficult to perform in real-time. In this work, we explore the efficacy of microwave hyperthermia in various inhomogeneous media under changing dielectric conditions, with the goal of informing the design of future adaptive real-time microwave hyperthermia techniques. We aim to achieve cell apoptosis by obtaining temperatures of $\sim$ 45 $^\circ\text{C}$ through selective absorption of electromagnetic wave focusing at a 2.5 GHz carrier frequency with little to no knowledge of the changes in the dielectric media and simultaneously place nulls to avoid unwanted heating outside of the treatment zone. We investigate the effectiveness of the linear constrained minimum power (LCMP) algorithm for near-field multi-objective beamforming and examine the power density obtained from finite-difference time-domain (FDTD) simulations on simple analytical models and anatomically realistic numerical breast phantoms. To gain a comprehensive knowledge of the efficacy of the beamformer we evaluate the resulting thermal maps of the models in simple homogeneous cases, heterogeneous cases and MRI-derived phantom breast models.

### [3] [The Resurrection of Spectrum Spreading for 6G and Beyond: From Sinusoids to Chirps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00249)
*Hyeon Seok Rou,Giuseppe Thadeu Freitas de Abreu,Emil Björnson,Sunwoo Kim,Marios Kountouris*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对高速移动和毫米波/亚THz频段带来的双弥散信道挑战，传统OFDM的正弦子载波缺乏多普勒鲁棒性。文章对比了延迟-多普勒域（OTFS）和参数化chirp（AFDM）两种时频扩展方案，认为基于chirp的子载波能在保留OFDM基础设施的同时提升多普勒鲁棒性，并适用于ISAC等新兴技术，是未来物理层波形演进的合理方向。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着未来无线系统向高速移动应用和毫米波/亚THz频段迁移，信道呈现双弥散特性，OFDM所依赖的时不变假设难以维持，多普勒引起的性能退化日益严重。现有增强技术（MIMO、先进编码、调度）仅提供增量改进且引入额外开销，根本原因在于正弦子载波本身缺乏波形级的多普勒鲁棒性。

Method: 文章系统分析了两种时频扩展范式：1）通过延迟-多普勒（DD）域实现的二维各向同性扩展，以正交时频空（OTFS）为代表；2）基于可参数化chirp的剪切扩展，以仿射频分复用（AFDM）为代表，并对比了它们在双弥散信道下的设计考量。

Result: 分析表明，从OFDM的正弦子载波转向基于chirp的子载波设计，能够在保留现有OFDM基础设施的同时，显著提升多普勒鲁棒性，并为集成感知与通信（ISAC）等新兴物理层技术提供良好的可扩展性。

Conclusion: 因此，从正弦到chirp的波形演进不仅是技术驱动的必然选择，更是未来无线物理层设计具有前景的演进方向，能够同时满足双弥散环境下的鲁棒性需求和ISAC等多功能一体化需求。

Abstract: Orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing (OFDM) and its static sinusoidal subcarriers have underpinned the 4G and 5G eras, delivering high spectral efficiency and resilience to multipath fading through an efficient multicarrier architecture. However, as future systems move toward doubly dispersive environments driven by high-mobility applications and migration to mmWave/sub-THz bands, the time-invariance assumption underlying OFDM becomes increasingly difficult to maintain, and Doppler-induced degradation becomes prominent. While enhancements such as MIMO, advanced coding, and scheduling provide incremental remedies, they introduce additional overhead, because the sinusoidal subcarrier itself offers no inherent waveform-level robustness to Doppler impairments. Accordingly, two time-frequency spreading philosophies have emerged to improve Doppler resilience by distributing each symbol's energy across both dimensions of the time-frequency plane: (i) 2D isotropic spreading via the delay-Doppler (DD) domain, exemplified by the orthogonal time frequency space (OTFS) family, and (ii) sheared spreading via parameterizable chirps, exemplified by the affine frequency-division multiplexing (AFDM) family. In this article, we examine key considerations for future waveform design across these paradigms and argue that transitioning from the sinusoidal subcarriers of OFDM to the chirp-based subcarriers offers a viable design direction for improving Doppler robustness while retaining much of the mature OFDM infrastructure. This perspective also highlights the suitability of chirp-based waveforms for integrated sensing and communications (ISAC) and their extensibility to emerging physical-layer techniques. Overall, we argue that the transition from sinusoids to chirps is a technically motivated, compelling evolutionary direction for future wireless physical layer design.

Abstract: Modeling signals as linear combinations of atoms from a dictionary is ubiquitous in modern signal processing. Numerical experiments corroborate the theoretical predictions and demonstrate the practical relevance of the proposed framework.
