### [1] [Impact of Background Dense Multipath Components on Multi-Band Fusion ISAC Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27265)
*Dexin Wang,Roberto Bomfin,Ahmad Bazzi,Marwa Chafii*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文针对6G集成感知与通信（ISAC）系统中的多频段感知问题，研究了密集多径分量（DMC）对多频段融合性能的影响。通过分析克拉默-拉奥下界（CRB）和提出的多频段估计器，数值结果表明在多径主导场景中，多频段处理能显著降低估计误差并提升系统鲁棒性。

Motivation: 随着6G IMT-2030标准的推进，集成感知与通信（ISAC）成为六大核心应用场景之一。频率范围3（FR3，7-24 GHz）的引入带来了非连续子频段，使多频段操作的重要性更加突出。然而，频率相关的杂波（即密集多径分量DMC）成为关键挑战，亟需研究其对多频段ISAC系统性能的影响。

Method: 本文在前期文献和实验观测的基础上，采用理论分析与数值仿真相结合的方法。首先推导了基于克拉默-拉奥下界（CRB）的理论性能极限，然后提出了专门的多频段估计算法，并通过数值实验评估了在DMC环境下的性能表现。

Conclusion: 本研究证实了在6G ISAC系统中，多频段感知是应对密集多径挑战的有效方案。所提出的多频段估计器和CRB分析为系统设计提供了理论指导，强调了多频段融合在提升感知性能方面的重要性，为未来6G ISAC系统的实际部署提供了有价值的参考。

Abstract: Multi-band sensing has emerged as a key enabler of integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), one of the six primary usage scenarios defined for IMT-2030 (6G). The introduction of frequency range 3 (FR3, 7-24 GHz), comprising non-contiguous sub-bands across a wide frequency span, further reinforces the importance of multi-band operation. In such scenarios, frequency-dependent clutter, collectively referred to as dense multipath components (DMC), must be carefully considered. Building on prior literature and our experimental observations, this paper analyzes the impact of DMC on multi-band fusion ISAC systems by investigating Cramér-Rao bound (CRB)-based fundamental limits and the performance of our proposed multi-band estimator. Numerical results show that multi-band processing, especially in DMC-dominated scenarios, can substantially reduce estimation error and boost system resilience when channel statistics vary.

### [2] [Array Zooming Optimization for Near-Field Localization With Movable Antennas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27352)
*Yuxin Duan,Boyu Teng,Xiaojun Yuan,Rui Wang*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对稀疏阵列近场定位中分辨率与空间模糊的矛盾，本文提出一种可动天线使能的阵列缩放系统。该系统通过动态调整天线间距融合多测量信息，减轻混叠效应；理论分析假峰分布并推导包含虚警概率的更紧性能下界；设计优化算法抑制假峰并最小化定位误差。数值结果表明该策略在不同信噪比下自适应优化阵列配置，定位精度显著优于固定间距阵列和CRB基准。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 近场定位中，稀疏阵列虽可实现高分辨率与低成本，但会因欠采样引入空间模糊（混叠效应），导致分辨率与模糊性之间存在固有矛盾。传统固定间距阵列无法动态适应不同信噪比环境，难以在保持分辨率的同时抑制虚假峰值，亟需一种自适应阵列重构方案突破性能瓶颈。

Method: 1) 设计多测量阵列缩放系统，利用可动天线的空间自由度动态调整天线间距，融合不同构型的观测信息以抑制空间混叠；2) 理论分析稀疏近场感知中的多峰分布特性，推导假峰分布模型及包含虚警概率的更紧性能下界；3) 针对实际多峰分布中存在的多个假峰，提出阵列缩放优化算法，实现假峰抑制与定位误差最小化。

Result: 大量数值结果表明：所提AZ策略能够在不同信噪比条件下自适应优化阵列配置，有效缓解空间混叠并维持高空间分辨率。与固定间距阵列和基于CRB的阵列缩放基准相比，该方案在定位精度上实现显著提升，验证了理论分析的有效性与优化算法的优越性。

Conclusion: 可动天线使能的阵列缩放系统通过动态重构阵列几何与多测量信息融合，成功解决了稀疏阵列近场定位的分辨率-模糊性困境。理论推导的假峰分布模型与性能下界为系统优化提供了精确指导，数值结果证实该方案具有卓越的自适应能力与定位性能，为高精度、低成本无线感知提供了新思路。

Abstract: The emergence of movable antenna (MA) technology provides a promising way to enhance wireless sensing and communication by introducing spatial degrees of freedom through dynamic array reconfiguration. In near-field localization, achieving high resolution at low cost necessitates the adoption of sparse arrays. However, such sparsity tends to introduce spatial ambiguity due to aliasing effects. To resolve this resolution-ambiguity dilemma, this paper proposes an MA-enabled array zooming (AZ) system. First, we design a multi-measurement array zooming system that dynamically adjusts antenna spacings. By fusing the observational information from different measurements, the proposed AZ system effectively mitigates spatial aliasing while maintaining spatial resolution. Second, to quantify the performance limits under the severe multi-modal distributions inherent in sparse near-field sensing, we theoretically analyze the false peak distribution and derive a tighter performance lower bound, which incorporates the false detection probability. Third, considering that multiple false peaks may exist in practical multi-modal distributions, we propose an optimization algorithm for the AZ system to suppress false peaks and minimize the localization error. Extensive numerical results demonstrate that the proposed AZ strategy adaptively optimizes array configurations under varying signal-to-noise ratios (SNRs), substantially outperforming both conventional fixed-spacing arrays and Cramer-Rao bound (CRB)-based AZ benchmarks in localization accuracy.

### [3] [Semantics-Aware Hierarchical Token Communication: Clustering, Bit Mapping, and Power Allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27641)
*Jihoon Lee,Seungeun Oh,Jihong Park,Seong-Lyun Kim,Seung-Woo Ko*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对现有令牌通信物理层设计忽略语义结构导致低信噪比下语义损失的问题，本文提出分层令牌通信H-TokCom框架，通过将语义相似令牌聚类并分层编码（簇前缀+令牌后缀），结合非均匀功率分配，在COCO数据集上实现3dB信噪比时35.4%的语义相似度增益。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有令牌通信虽超越传统比特通信范式，但其物理层设计（令牌-比特映射和功率分配）仍沿用传统方式，未能反映令牌级语义，导致低信噪比下严重的语义损失。

Method: 提出分层令牌通信H-TokCom：1）将语义相似令牌聚类；2）采用分层比特表示，每个令牌由簇级前缀和令牌特化后缀构成；3）为前缀比特分配更高发射功率，确保簇信息优先保护。

Conclusion: 通过将语义结构直接嵌入物理层设计，H-TokCom有效缓解了传统令牌通信的语义损失问题，为语义通信系统的物理层优化提供了新范式。

Abstract: Despite the rise of token communication (TokCom) as a new paradigm beyond traditional bit communication, existing approaches have primarily adopted artificial intelligence (AI)-centric designs that rely on semantic recovery via large models. Meanwhile, their physical-layer designs, such as token-bit mapping and power allocation, remain conventional and do not reflect token-level semantics. These semantics-agnostic designs can lead to significant semantic loss, particularly at low signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) levels. To address this issue, we propose hierarchical TokCom (H-TokCom), a framework that embeds semantic structure directly into physical-layer design. The key idea is to group semantically similar tokens into clusters and hierarchically assign their bit representations, where each token is represented by a cluster-level prefix and a token-specific suffix. As long as the cluster bits are correctly delivered, errors in the suffix bits typically map the received token to another within the same semantic cluster, resulting in only limited semantic distortion. This robustness is further strengthened by allocating more transmit power to the prefix bits than to the suffix bits. Simulation results show that H-TokCom achieves substantial semantic-similarity gains over conventional TokCom across the considered SNR range, increasing the semantic similarity from $0.206$ to $0.279$ at $γ=3$ dB on COCO, corresponding to a gain of $0.073$ $(35.4\%)$.
