### [1] [Waveform Design for Underwater Simultaneous Acoustic Information and Power Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01565)
*Jinheng Kang,Yizhe Zhao,Kun Yang,Jun Liu*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文针对水下物联网，提出了一种联合优化波形与功率分配的多载波SAIPT系统，通过考虑换能器非线性和整流器特性，显著提升了水下声学能量传输的效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为了实现水下物联网（IoUT）的自维持和免维护运行，需要解决水下环境能量获取困难的问题，同时考虑到实际声学换能器的频率依赖特性和非线性整流电路对能量传输效率的影响，研究一套高效的声学信息与功率同步传输（SAIPT）方案。

Method: 1. 建立包含声学换能器频率响应和整流器非线性特性的系统模型；2. 针对APT场景，采用连续凸逼近（SCA）方法优化波形向量；3. 针对SAIPT场景，提出基于SCA的交替优化（AO）框架，联合优化功率分配因子和波形向量。

Result: 仿真结果表明，通过整合换能器频率响应、整流器非线性及多载波波形的高峰均功率比（PAPR）特性，该方法显著提高了声学能量传输效率；此外，合理配置子载波数量和子载波间距等参数，能够进一步优化直流（DC）输出功率。

Conclusion: 该研究成功建立了一个考虑声学换能器频率响应和整流电路非线性的多载波水下SAIPT系统优化模型。研究证明了通过联合优化波形向量与功率分配因子，能够显著提升能量传输效率，为实现自维持的IoUT网络提供了重要的理论指导与设计策略。

Abstract: Simultaneous acoustic information and power transfer (SAIPT) plays a crucial role in enabling self-sustainable and maintenance-free Internet of Underwater Things (IoUT) networks. This paper studies a multicarrier underwater SAIPT system that jointly considers the frequency-dependent characteristics of acoustic transducers and the nonlinear behavior of rectifier circuits. The waveform vector is firstly optimized using the successive convex approximation (SCA) method under constraints on average and peak transmit power for acoustic power transfer (APT). Then, in the SAIPT scenario, both the power splitting factor and waveform vectors are jointly optimized through an alternating optimization (AO) framework based on SCA, subject to transmit power and achievable rate constraints. Simulation results demonstrate that incorporating the transducer's frequency response, rectifier nonlinearity, and the high peak-to-average power ratio (PAPR) of multicarrier waveforms leads to a significant improvement in acoustic energy transfer efficiency. The results also show that the energy harvesting DC output can be further enhanced by properly choosing system parameters, such as the number of subcarriers and subcarrier spacing.

### [2] [Scene-Conditioned PINN-GNN for Multipath RF Maps: Cross-Scene Generation and In-Scene Completion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01777)
*Lizhou Liu,Xiaohui Chen,Zihan Tang,Mengyao Ma,Wenyi Zhang*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种结合物理驱动神经网络（PINN）与图神经网络（GNN）的新型射频地图构建框架，通过物理约束与空间关联性建模，有效实现了复杂环境下的高保真多径传播特性重构与跨场景应用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 针对传统无线环境建模在稀疏观测下的精度不足以及现有数据驱动方法缺乏物理约束、难以泛化至不同场景的问题，旨在构建一个既能利用物理规律又具备空间建模能力的通用射频地图构建框架。

Method: 提出了一种融合物理驱动神经网络（PINN）与图神经网络（GNN）的统一框架。PINN被用于嵌入电磁传播物理约束，建立从接收器位置到路径增益、到达时间及角度等参数的映射；GNN通过建模接收器间的空间关联性来增强预测的空间一致性；同时引入了一种峰值加权动态时间规整（Peak-weighted DTW）指标，用于评估信道脉冲响应的重构质量。

Result: 实验结果表明，该方法在地图级和多径级评价指标上均显著优于图像处理、扩散模型及插值等基准方法，在稀疏观测条件下展现出极强的鲁棒性、泛化能力及高保真重构性能。

Conclusion: 本研究提出的基于物理驱动神经网络（PINN）与图神经网络（GNN）的统一框架，成功实现了多径参数的精准建模与空间一致性约束，证明了物理先验与图拓扑在射频地图构建中的重要价值，为复杂无线环境下的感知与优化提供了有力工具。

Abstract: Radio frequency (RF) maps provide a compact representation of multipath propagation characteristics and are fundamental to channel modeling, coverage analysis, and environment-aware wireless optimization. This paper proposes a unified RF map construction framework based on a physics-informed neural network (PINN) and a graph neural network (GNN), supporting both cross-scene generation and in-scene completion with 2D and 2.5D environmental representations. The PINN embeds electromagnetic propagation constraints to establish a physically consistent mapping from receiver locations to multipath parameters, including path gain, time of arrival, and angles, while the GNN enforces spatial consistency by modeling correlations among neighboring receivers. To comprehensively evaluate multipath reconstruction quality, we propose a peak-weighted dynamic time warping metric that jointly accounts for amplitude errors and peak delay misalignment in channel impulse responses. Extensive experiments demonstrate that the proposed method consistently outperforms image-based, diffusion-based, and interpolation baselines across both map-level and multipath-level metrics, achieving robust generalization and high-fidelity RF map construction under sparse observations.

### [3] [Fourier Preconditioning for Neural Feature Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02199)
*Preston Pitzer,Anish Pradhan,Harpreet S. Dhillon*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对H-Score特征学习在受限网络中对输入基敏感的问题，本文提出了利用傅里叶变换（FFT）进行酉预处理的方法，有效降低了截断误差，并在资源受限场景下显著提升了性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 互信息（MI）驱动的特征学习虽然有效，但在小样本情况下估计噪声大。H-Score作为一种基于二阶统计量的MI代理指标虽然更为稳健，但在受限近似函数类中对输入基旋转敏感。因此，研究如何通过基变换优化H-Score网络的性能，以提升在资源受限环境下的特征提取能力具有重要意义。

Method: 1. 理论分析：证明了H-Score在不受限函数空间下的不变性及其在受限函数类下对基旋转的敏感性；2. 预处理策略：引入酉预处理技术，利用FFT作为一种数据无关、低成本的预处理手段，将预测依赖集中在少数主导模态中；3. 评估指标：开发了基于谱熵和累积依赖能量的无需训练指标，用于预评估基变换的有效性。

Result: 在八个多变量数据集上的实验表明，FFT预处理在资源受限场景下表现出色，归一化均方误差（NMSE）降低幅度高达50%。同时，提出的无需训练指标能够准确量化基的适用性，并有效预测下游推理的性能增益。

Conclusion: 本文提出了一种基于酉预处理（特别是傅里叶变换）的方法来优化H-Score特征学习。通过选择合适的基，可以有效减少有限宽度网络的截断误差，并提出了一套无需训练的度量指标来预测性能收益。这一方法在资源受限的环境下具有显著的实际应用价值。

Abstract: Mutual information (MI)-inspired feature learning techniques are capable of generating low-dimensional embeddings that retain nonlinear dependence structures, but direct estimations of MI suffer from noisy probability distribution estimates in the low-data regime. The H-Score objective, computed from second-order statistics, provides a practical proxy metric for training feature extraction networks. We prove that H-Score is invariant to invertible transformations in the unrestricted functional setting, but becomes sensitive to input basis rotations under constrained approximation classes. Consequently, we study unitary preconditioning for H-Score networks and show that selecting an appropriate basis rotation reduces finite-width truncation error by concentrating predictive dependence into fewer dominant modes. We identify the fast Fourier transform (FFT) as an effective data-independent, low-cost preconditioner for approximately stationary processes, where spectral structure induces concentration of the cross-covariance singular value spectrum. We introduce training-free metrics based on spectral entropy and cumulative dependence energy to quantify basis suitability and predict downstream inference gains prior to network training. Experiments across eight multivariate datasets demonstrate that FFT preconditioning is particularly useful in resource-constrained regimes, achieving up to 50% normalized mean squared error (NMSE) reduction, while the proposed metrics correlate with observed performance gains and correctly identify cases where spectral preconditioning is detrimental.
