### [1] [A Stochastic Optimization Framework for RIS-Aided Wireless Network Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28018)
*Davide Gagliardi,Alessio Zappone,Domenico Ciuonzo,Marco Di Renzo*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于连续随机优化（CE和MH方法）的框架，用于解决RIS辅助无线网络中复杂的大规模非凸优化问题，在保证收敛性的同时显著降低了计算开销。

Motivation: RIS优化问题通常表现为大规模、非凸的复杂数学问题，随着散射单元数量的增加，传统确定性算法的计算复杂度和优化效率面临巨大挑战，需要更高效的优化手段。

Method: 提出了一种基于连续交叉熵（CE）和Metropolis-Hastings（MH）方法的随机优化框架，通过直接处理连续变量并结合松弛与投影技术，实现对近无源和有源RIS配置的优化。

Result: 数值结果表明，该方法在可达速率最大化和能效最大化场景下，性能达到或优于现有最先进的确定性算法，且在典型场景下的执行时间缩短至原来的十分之一。

Conclusion: 本文提出的基于连续交叉熵（CE）和Metropolis-Hastings（MH）的随机优化框架，为RIS辅助无线网络提供了一种高效且具备收敛保障的求解方案，在处理高维非凸优化问题上展现了比确定性算法更强的鲁棒性和更快的收敛速度。

Abstract: Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RISs) are a promising technology for improving the spectral and energy efficiency of future wireless networks, which make use of metasurfaces. However, optimizing RIS configurations typically leads to large-scale, non-convex problems whose complexity grows significantly with the number of scattering elements and the adoption of advanced metasurface architectures. In this paper, we develop a stochastic optimization framework for RIS-aided wireless networks based on continuous versions of the ($a$) cross-entropy (CE) and ($b$) Metropolis-Hastings (MH) methods. Unlike existing stochastic approaches that mainly focus on discrete optimization, the proposed framework directly handles continuous variables and can be readily applied to discrete settings through relaxation and projection. We provide a theoretical characterization of the proposed algorithms, including convergence guarantees and efficiency analysis. The framework is applied to ($i$) achievable-rate maximization with nearly-passive RISs and ($ii$) energy-efficiency maximization with active RISs. Numerical results show that the proposed methods achieve performance comparable to, or better than, state-of-the-art deterministic algorithms, while reducing execution times up to 10 times in representative scenarios.
