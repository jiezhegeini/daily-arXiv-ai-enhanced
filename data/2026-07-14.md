### [1] [Fast Pinching-Antenna Activation for AirComp](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08873)
*Zhenqiao Cheng,Boqun Zhao,Chongjun Ouyang,Xingqi Zhang*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种用于空中计算的夹持天线系统（PASS），通过构建离散天线激活优化模型，并结合秩一递归与高效搜索算法，显著提升了空中计算的精度与实现效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在空中计算（AirComp）场景中，传统的阵列天线设计难以满足实际部署需求，而夹持天线系统（PASS）通过在介质波导上离散选择天线位置，能够实现更灵活的信号处理，但如何在该离散受限条件下优化天线激活以最小化计算误差是亟待解决的挑战。

Method: 1. 将离散激活问题建模为最小化AirComp均方误差（MSE）的优化问题；2. 推导了基于逆Gram矩阵的MSE表达；3. 利用秩一递归（rank-one recursion）实现高效的MSE增量计算；4. 开发了贪婪搜索、束搜索以及基于一阶MSE近似的相干聚合搜索算法以降低计算复杂度。

Result: 数值仿真结果表明，所提出的PASS优化方案能够显著降低AirComp的均方误差，且所提的相干聚合搜索算法在低信噪比（SNR）下展现出近乎最优的性能，计算复杂度远低于传统穷举搜索。

Conclusion: 本研究提出的夹持天线系统（PASS）及配套的快速搜索算法，有效解决了空中计算（AirComp）中离散天线位置的选择难题，证明了该系统在提升计算精度方面优于传统天线阵列，具有显著的实用价值。

Abstract: A pinching-antenna system (PASS) is considered for over-the-air computation (AirComp). Multiple dielectric waveguides are deployed at the base station, and one pinching antenna (PA) is activated on each waveguide. For practical implementation, each PA is restricted to a finite set of preconfigured locations. The resulting discrete activation problem is formulated to minimize the AirComp mean-squared error (MSE). After the optimal aggregation vector is derived, the minimum MSE is expressed through an inverse Gram matrix. A rank-one recursion is derived to evaluate the exact MSE reduction produced by each candidate. Greedy search and beam search are then developed for fast tree search. To further reduce complexity, coherent aggregation search is proposed from a first-order MSE approximation. It admits a separable closed-form selection rule and is asymptotically optimal in the low signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) regime. Numerical results show that the proposed methods substantially improve the AirComp accuracy of PASS over conventional antenna arrays.
