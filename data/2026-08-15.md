Abstract: This letter proposes the integration of on-off digital noise (OODN) modulation with fluid antenna systems (FASs). A unified analytical framework is developed to evaluate the performance of FAS- assisted OODN receivers over additive white Gaussian noise and generalized \k{appa}-μ fading channels.
