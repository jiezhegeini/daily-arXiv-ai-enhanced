### [1] [The Radon Transform, True Time Delay Beamforming, and Ultra-Wideband Antenna Arrays (Invited Paper)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21022)
*Tyler Ikehara,Ibrahim Pehlivan,Danijela Cabric,Thomas L. Marzetta*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对6G FR3频段超大带宽与大型阵列导致的波束发散问题，本文利用Radon变换实现真实时延波束成形，通过semblance检测和慢度滤波分离近/远场信号，结合子阵列三角定位与双曲线积分，完成近场源坐标估计与信号包络提取。

Motivation: FR3频段作为6G研究核心，其极端带宽与物理大阵列使传统相移波束成形产生严重波束发散(beam-squint)和相干性损失，亟需真实时延波束成形(TTD)方案，并缺乏有效的空间/时间测量数据处理理论框架。

Method: 1) 基于Radon变换与TTD的数学等价性，引入CT与地震勘探技术；2) 采用semblance检测与Radon慢度滤波剔除远场信号，分离近场成分；3) 阵列分区后，利用semblance Radon变换估计各子阵列信号到达角；4) 通过三角定位求解近场源空间坐标；5) 沿双曲线轨迹积分原始数据提取个体近场信号包络。

Result: 成功构建Radon变换驱动的TTD处理体系，实现远场干扰抑制、近场信号分离、到达角精确估计、源坐标定位及信号包络提取的完整流程，为FR3频段大阵列系统提供理论基础。

Conclusion: Radon变换为6G FR3频段真实时延波束成形提供了跨学科理论支撑，通过借鉴成熟的成像与勘探技术，有效解决了超宽带大阵列的波束发散挑战，实现了近场信号的高精度空间定位与参数提取。

Abstract: The FR3 band has emerged as the major focus of 6G wireless research. FR3 cellular operation presents the challenge of extreme bandwidth combined with physically large antenna arrays. In this regime, conventional phase-shift beamforming entails a loss of coherence (beam-squint), and has to be replaced by true time delay beamforming (TTD). It happens that TTD is mathematically equivalent to taking the Radon transform of the space/time measurements. We exploit fifty years of research in the application of the Radon transform to computer tomography and to seismic exploration to elucidate the workings of TTD. We use the Radon transform combined with semblance detection and Radon slowness filtering to remove far-field signals from the measured space/time signals from a linear array, leaving only near-field signals. In turn we partition the array into sub-arrays. For each sub-array we estimate, via the semblance Radon transform, the angles-of-arrival of the near-field signals. We then use triangulation to estimate the coordinates of the near-field sources. Finally we integrate the original space/time data along hyperbolic trajectories to extract the individual near-field signal envelopes.

### [2] [Complex Approximate Message Passing with Non-separable Denoising](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21115)
*Vishnu Teja Kunde,Alessandro Mirri,Jean-Francois Chamberland,Enrico Paolini*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文建立了复数域中非可分离去噪函数的近似消息传递（AMP）的统一状态演化理论。通过构造增广实值系统将问题提升到高维空间，再通过多对一规范变换恢复复数域，使得Onsager校正自然涉及Wirtinger导数，且状态演化可简化为标量复数递归。该方法扩展至矩阵值情形，可处理多特征向量联合结构约束，并通过复数稀疏组LASSO在OTFS随机接入前导检测中的应用验证了有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尽管矩阵AMP、复数AMP及非可分离函数AMP已有独立进展，但复数域与非可分离去噪器结合的AMP缺乏统一的状态演化理论，限制了其在复数信号处理中的理论分析和性能预测能力。

Method: 提出一种增广实值系统构造方法，通过将复数域问题提升到高维实值空间，再应用多对一规范变换映射回复数域。在此框架下，Onsager校正项自然引入Wirtinger导数，而状态演化方程可简化为标量复数递归形式，从而统一了复数与非可分离结构的理论分析。

Result: 成功建立了复数非可分离AMP的状态演化理论，证明其可退化为标量复数递归；扩展至矩阵值设置，支持多特征向量联合稀疏结构；数值实验验证了状态演化对性能的准确预测能力，并显示复数非可分离去噪相比可分离和实值方法具有显著性能提升。

Conclusion: 本文填补了复数AMP与非可分离去噪器结合的理论空白，为复数域高维推断问题提供了统一的性能分析工具。该框架在OTFS通信等复数信号处理应用中展现出优越性，为未来复数域AMP的理论发展和实际应用奠定了基础。

Abstract: Approximate Message Passing (AMP) is a general framework for iterative algorithms, originally developed for compressed sensing and later extended to a wide range of high-dimensional inference problems. Although recent work has advanced matrix AMP, complex AMP, and AMP for non-separable functions independently, a unified state evolution theory for complex AMP with non-separable denoisers has been lacking. This article fills that gap by establishing state evolution in the setting of complex, non-separable denoising functions. The proposed approach constructs an augmented real-valued system that lifts the problem to a higher-dimensional space, then recovers the complex domain through a many-to-one canonical transformation. Under this construction, the Onsager correction naturally involves Wirtinger derivatives, and the resulting state evolution reduces to scalar complex recursions despite the non-separable structure of the denoisers. The framework extends to the matrix-valued setting, accommodating multiple feature vectors simultaneously. This generalization enables AMP to exploit joint structural constraints, such as simultaneous group and element sparsity, in complex-valued recovery problems. The complex sparse group least absolute shrinkage and selection operator (LASSO) serves as a key instantiation, motivated by preamble detection in Orthogonal Time-Frequency Space (OTFS)-based unsourced random access. Numerical experiments confirm that state evolution accurately predicts performance and show that complex non-separable denoising can produce significant gains over separable and real-valued alternatives.

### [3] [Threat Detection and Resilience Techniques in PRS-Assisted OTDOA 5G Positioning Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21126)

Simulation results demonstrate that while encryption, authentication schemes, and tracking robustly counter selective PRS spoofing and jamming, the proposed spatial and cross-layer mechanisms are essential for detecting meaconing, collectively maintaining attack detection rates in excess of 90% while keeping false alarm rates minimal.

### [4] [A Hidden Markov Framework for Physically Interpretable Arc Stability Dynamics in Welding Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21839)
*Hidir Selcuk Nogay*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本研究针对电弧焊过程的非平稳时变特性，提出一种基于隐马尔可夫模型的时序建模框架。通过短时傅里叶变换提取电流信号的频域特征（能量、熵、质心），将电弧动态过程建模为潜在工况序列，成功识别出瞬态、稳定和熄灭三种主导状态，揭示了频谱能量单调递增与熵值递减的稳定化规律，验证了时序建模相比静态分类在电弧稳定性评估中的优越性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统基于帧的电弧稳定性评估方法难以有效处理电弧焊过程的强非平稳性和时变特性，导致评估结果不可靠。亟需一种能够捕捉时序演化规律、具有物理可解释性的动态建模方法来实现对电弧状态演变的准确追踪。

Method: 研究采用概率状态空间框架：首先通过短时傅里叶变换将焊接电流信号转换至时频域；其次提取能量、熵和质心等具有物理意义的谱描述符构建观测序列；最后利用隐马尔可夫模型捕获时序依赖关系，估计电弧状态的演化过程。

Result: 分析揭示了三种主导工况状态（瞬态、稳定、熄灭），稳定状态下频谱能量单调递增而熵值递减，表明变异性降低。尽管特征空间存在部分重叠，但推断的状态序列表现出强时间相干性，状态持续性高且转移率低，验证了模型的有效性。

Conclusion: 研究强调了时序建模在电弧行为分析中的重要性，克服了静态分类的局限性。所提框架提供了可解释且物理一致的电弧行为表征，为实现焊接过程稳定性的动态监测与分析奠定了更现实的基础。

Abstract: Electric arc welding (EAW) exhibits strongly non stationary and temporally evolving behavior, making reliable assessment of arc stability difficult using conventional frame based approaches. In this study, arc dynamics are modeled as a sequence of latent operational regimes within a probabilistic state-space framework. The welding current signal is transformed into a time-frequency domain using Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT), and a set of physically meaningful spectral descriptors, including energy, entropy, and centroid, is extracted to construct the observation sequence. A Hidden Markov Model (HMM) is employed to capture temporal dependencies and estimate the evolution of arc states. The analysis reveals three dominant regimes, transient, stable, and extinction, with a clear monotonic increase in spectral energy and a corresponding decrease in entropy, indicating reduced variability under stable conditions. Despite partial overlap in the feature space, the inferred state sequence exhibits strong temporal coherence, supported by high state persistence and low transition rates. These findings highlight the limitations of static classification and emphasize the importance of temporal modeling. The proposed framework provides an interpretable and physically consistent representation of arc behavior, enabling more realistic monitoring and analysis of stability dynamics in welding processes.
