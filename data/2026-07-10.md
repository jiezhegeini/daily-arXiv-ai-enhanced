### [1] [Entropy-Adaptive Multi-Map Chaotic Modulation for Physical-Layer Security](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07828)
*Dhrumil Bhatt,Aarish Patel*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 该论文提出了一种基于多映射混沌调制与熵动态调节的自适应物理层安全框架，通过异构混沌映射与入侵感知机制，有效提升了多用户无线系统在复杂环境下的抗窃听能力与安全性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统混沌通信方案依赖静态参数保密，容易受到重构攻击。为了解决这一局限，研究旨在开发一种能够将信息熵作为可调节安全变量，并能根据环境动态适应的物理层安全架构，以提升多用户无线系统的安全性。

Method: 提出了一种名为MU-DE-IAEACM-MM的框架，核心机制包括：1. 利用Logistic、Tent、Chebyshev和Sine等异构混沌映射增加熵维度；2. 引入熵驱动的自适应律动态更新混沌参数；3. 结合基于相关性的入侵度量机制，在检测到潜在窃听攻击时触发熵的动态提升。

Result: 蒙特卡洛仿真显示，该框架在多种噪声和干扰环境下，显著扩大了合法接收机与非法接收机之间的误码率（BER）差距，并在高密度部署（高达64个合法用户和30个窃听者）下依然保持了正的安全容量增益，验证了其稳定性和有效性。

Conclusion: 研究表明，基于熵调节的多映射混沌调制框架为下一代无线通信和物联网提供了可扩展且同步稳定的物理层安全解决方案，能有效应对高密度用户场景下的安全挑战。

Abstract: This paper presents a Multi-Map Dynamic-Entropy Intrusion-Aware Chaotic Modulation (MU-DE-IAEACM-MM) framework for adaptive physical-layer security in multi-user wireless systems. Unlike conventional chaos-based schemes that rely on static parameter secrecy, the proposed architecture treats entropy as a regulated security variable and dynamically updates chaotic control parameters using an entropy-driven adaptation law. Heterogeneous chaotic maps, including Logistic, Tent, Chebyshev, and Sine generators, are distributed across users to enlarge entropy dimensionality and reduce cross-user statistical dependence. A correlation-based intrusion metric is incorporated to detect improved adversarial reconstruction coherence and trigger controlled entropy escalation. Stability analysis establishes bounded convergence conditions for the adaptive update process. Monte Carlo simulations under AWGN, fading, impulsive, colored, and narrowband interference demonstrate a persistent BER gap between legitimate and mismatched receivers and measurable secrecy capacity gains over representative fixed-parameter chaotic modulation schemes. The framework maintains positive secrecy rates in dense deployments with up to 64 legitimate users and 30 passive eavesdroppers. The results indicate that entropy-regulated multi-map chaotic modulation provides a scalable and synchronisation-stable approach for adaptive physical-layer security in next-generation wireless and IoT networks.

### [2] [Fundamental Sensing Limits of 6G Cooperative MIMO-ISAC Networks: Joint Position-Velocity CRLB and Decoupling Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08510)
*Yanpeng Su,Norman Franchi,Maximilian Lübke*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文研究了合作式ISAC网络中的性能边界，通过推导精确的CRLB并提出一种低复杂度的简化方案，解决了定位与测速参数耦合带来的计算难题，为资源分配与波束赋形等实际优化问题提供了高效、可靠的性能参考指标。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的合作式集成感知与通信（ISAC）研究大多忽略了费舍尔信息矩阵中位置与速度参数之间的耦合影响，且复杂的精确CRLB计算在实际网络资源优化（如波束赋形）中计算成本过高，缺乏可操作性。因此，需要推导更精确的CRLB并探索计算上更高效的简化模型。

Method: 本文首先推导了信号级参数到目标状态参数（位置和速度）的CRLB转换；其次，构建了包含位置与速度耦合项的完整FIM及精确CRLB，涵盖多单基地、多双基地及其混合传感模式；最后，提出了忽略耦合项的简化CRLB计算方法，并给出了该简化的有效性判据。

Result: 仿真结果表明，本文提出的简化CRLB与精确CRLB在多数通用场景下吻合度良好。研究还量化了资源配置与几何参数对定位和测速误差边界的影响，验证了简化CRLB作为可靠性能度量指标的有效性，并明确了其适用范围。

Conclusion: 该研究通过推导合作式ISAC网络中的完整费舍尔信息矩阵（FIM）及其对应的Cramér-Rao下界（CRLB），揭示了位置与速度之间的耦合关系，并提出了简化版的CRLB。简化版CRLB在保持高计算效率的同时，能够准确地作为资源分配和波束赋形等优化问题的性能评估指标，在通用场景下具有很高的适用性。

Abstract: This paper presents a Cramér-Rao lower bound (CRLB)-based performance bound analysis of cooperative multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) integrated sensing and communications (ISAC) networks. We first show the CRLB transformation of the signal-level parameters to the state parameters (position and velocity) in cooperative ISAC networks. Unlike existing studies that primarily ignored coupling between position and velocity in the Fisher information matrix (FIM), we derive the full FIM and the corresponding exact CRLB. Particularly, the results of multi-monostatic sensing, multi-bistatic sensing, and their hybrid are discussed. Addressing the complexity and tractability, we simplify the FIM and CRLB by excluding the coupling terms between the position and velocity, and provide a criterion for determining whether the simplification is valid. The simplified CRLB benefits from low computational complexity and provides a tractable and reliable performance metric for optimization problems such as resource allocation and beamforming. Finally, the position and velocity CRLBs and the simplification-induced error are examined in the simulation. The results demonstrate that the simplified CRLB can be applied in general cases. Based on the simulation results, the impact of resource and geometric parameters on position and velocity error bounds, and the validity of the simplified CRLBs is explained through the corresponding CRLB expressions.
