### [1] [Energy Efficient Active Stacked Intelligent Metasurfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15654)
*Li-Hsiang Shen*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种主动堆叠智能超表面（ASIM）辅助的下行传输框架，通过结合主动放大与多层架构，利用交替优化算法（SCA与贝叶斯优化）联合调控基站波束与超表面配置，有效提升了系统的整体能效。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 针对传统无源智能超表面在电磁波调控能力上的限制，研究人员提出了主动堆叠智能超表面（ASIM）架构。本文旨在通过利用ASIM的主动放大与多层结构，通过联合优化基站波束赋形与ASIM参数，最大化下行传输系统的能效。

Method: 针对高度耦合且非凸的能效优化问题，本文首先利用上图法（Epigraph）、拉格朗日对偶变换和二次变换对原问题进行转化。随后，采用交替优化框架：利用连续凸近似（SCA）算法求解基站波束赋形子问题，并采用基于高斯过程代理模型的贝叶斯优化方法求解ASIM配置子问题。

Result: 数值结果表明，所提出的ASIM方案在能效表现上显著优于传统的无源智能超表面方案及其他启发式基准方法。此外，研究还量化了放大能力、超表面层数及层间距对系统性能的影响，为未来主动超表面无线网络的设计提供了指导。

Conclusion: 本文证明了主动堆叠智能超表面（ASIM）在提升无线通信网络能效方面的巨大潜力。通过多层架构与主动放大技术，ASIM能够突破传统无源表面的局限，为未来无线网络的设计提供了有效的理论框架和优化策略。

Abstract: This paper investigates an energy-efficient active stacked intelligent metasurfaces (ASIM)-assisted downlink transmission framework, where a multi-antenna base station (BS) serves multiple users through a multi-layer metasurface architecture. Unlike conventional passive intelligent surfaces, the considered ASIM employs active amplification and multiple transmissive layers to enhance electromagnetic wave manipulation. We aim to maximize the system energy efficiency (EE) by jointly optimizing the BS beamforming and ASIM configurations under user quality-of-service and amplification constraints. The resulting problem is highly coupled and non-convex due to the cascaded near-field channel and multi-layer metasurface structure. To address this challenge, we first transform the original problem through epigraph, Lagrangian dual, and quadratic transformations. An alternative optimization framework is then developed, where the BS beamforming subproblem is solved via successive convex approximation (SCA), while the ASIM configuration is optimized using Bayesian optimization based on a Gaussian-process surrogate model. Numerical results demonstrate that the proposed scheme significantly improves the achievable EE compared to conventional passive SIM and heuristic benchmark methods. Furthermore, the impacts of amplification capability, number of metasurface layers, and inter-layer spacing on system performance are investigated, providing useful design insights for future active metasurface-assisted wireless networks.

### [2] [Map as a Prompt: Learning Multi-Modal Spatial-Signal Foundation Models for Cross-scenario Wireless Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15713)
*Yong Chu,Xun Zhou,Zenglin Xu,Hui Wang,Yue Yu*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: SigMap 是一款多模态无线定位基础模型，通过引入周期自适应掩码与地理信息提示增强，显著提升了定位的鲁棒性及跨场景的零样本泛化能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的数据驱动无线定位方法泛化能力较差，严重依赖海量标注数据，且难以适应新环境，无法满足 5G/6G 应用（如自动驾驶、XR）对鲁棒性和高精度的需求。

Method: 1. 提出循环自适应掩码策略（cycle-adaptive masking strategy）：根据无线信道的周期性特征动态调整掩码模式，以学习更鲁棒的无线信号特征表示；2. 提出“地图作为提示”框架（map-as-prompt）：通过轻量级软提示（soft prompts）将 3D 地理信息整合进模型，从而实现跨场景的高效适配。

Result: 实验表明，SigMap 在多项定位任务中达到了当前最优（SOTA）性能，并表现出强大的零样本（zero-shot）泛化能力，在未见过的环境中显著优于现有的有监督和自监督基线模型。

Conclusion: SigMap 证明了通过结合循环自适应掩码策略和“地图作为提示”（map-as-prompt）框架，多模态基础模型能够有效解决无线定位中泛化能力弱的问题，为 5G/6G 环境下的鲁棒定位提供了一种通用的解决方案。

Abstract: Accurate and robust wireless localization is a critical enabler for emerging 5G/6G applications, including autonomous driving, extended reality, and smart manufacturing. Despite its importance, achieving precise localization across diverse environments remains challenging due to the complex nature of wireless signals and their sensitivity to environmental changes. Existing data-driven approaches often suffer from limited generalization capability, requiring extensive labeled data and struggling to adapt to new scenarios. To address these limitations, we propose SigMap, a multimodal foundation model that introduces two key innovations: (1) A cycle-adaptive masking strategy that dynamically adjusts masking patterns based on channel periodicity characteristics to learn robust wireless representations; (2) A novel "map-as-prompt" framework that integrates 3D geographic information through lightweight soft prompts for effective cross-scenario adaptation. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our model achieves state-of-the-art performance across multiple localization tasks while exhibiting strong zero-shot generalization in unseen environments, significantly outperforming both supervised and self-supervised baselines by considerable margins.
