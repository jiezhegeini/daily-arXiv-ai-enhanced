Motivation: 旨在解决物联网（IoT）及机器通信场景中对低功耗、低复杂度通信的需求，通过将流体天线系统（FAS）与开关键控数字噪声（OODN）调制相结合，在保持非相干接收优势的同时，利用FAS的空间分集特性提升通信系统的可靠性。

Method: 研究团队开发了一个统一的分析框架，评估了在加性高斯白噪声（AWGN）和广义κ-μ衰落信道下，FAS辅助的OODN接收机的性能；分析过程综合考虑了流体天线端口选择、可用端口数量以及空间相关性，并推导了平均误码率（ABEP）的解析表达式及分集阶数。

Result: 通过蒙特卡洛仿真验证了理论分析的准确性。结果表明，FAS提供的空间分集效应能够显著增强OODN传输的可靠性，同时维持了系统低复杂度与非相干操作的固有特点。

Conclusion: 本文提出的FAS辅助OODN接收方案，在无需载波相位恢复的前提下，通过流体天线的空间分集显著提升了传输可靠性，为低功耗、低复杂度的物联网与机器通信系统提供了一种高效的物理层技术路径。

Abstract: This letter proposes the integration of on-off digital noise (OODN) modulation with fluid antenna systems (FASs). A unified analytical framework is developed to evaluate the performance of FAS- assisted OODN receivers over additive white Gaussian noise and generalized \k{appa}-μ fading channels. The analysis incorporates fluid antenna port selection, the number of available ports, and spatial correlation. Analytical expressions for the average bit error probability are derived and the achievable diversity order is characterized. All expressions are validated through Monte Carlo simulations. Results demonstrate that the spatial diversity provided by the FAS significantly enhances the reliability of OODN transmissions while preserving their inherent low-complexity, non-coherent operation without carrier-phase recovery. The proposed framework establishes a new research direction for energy-efficient Internet of Things (IoT) and machine-type communication systems.
