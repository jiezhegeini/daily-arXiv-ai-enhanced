### [1] [Multimodal Learning for MIMO Beam Prediction Based on Variational Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14650)
*Zijian Zheng,Wenqiang Yi,Hyundong Shin,Arumugam Nallanathan*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对集成感知与通信系统中波束预测需要大量对齐多模态数据的挑战，本文提出了一种基于变分推断的解耦框架，通过两阶段训练策略，仅使用20%的多模态数据即可达到与传统端到端方法相当的精度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在集成感知与通信的大规模MIMO系统中，准确的波束预测对于降低信令开销和延迟至关重要。虽然多模态学习可以利用不同传感器的互补信息来提高预测精度，但获取语义对齐的多模态数据集成本高昂，这限制了其在实际中的应用。

Method: 本文提出一个基于变分推断的多模态框架，将优化问题解耦为模块化特征提取和跨模态语义对齐两个部分。具体采用两阶段训练策略：首先在丰富的单模态数据上进行表示学习，然后对有限的多模态样本进行精细对齐。这种设计提高了数据效率，并确保在感知不确定性下的鲁棒特征融合。

Result: 在DeepSense6G数据集上的实验结果表明，所提出的框架在仅需20%多模态训练数据的情况下，就能达到具有竞争力的波束预测精度，并保持高可靠性，优于传统的端到端方法。

Conclusion: 该框架通过解耦设计和两阶段训练，有效解决了多模态数据稀缺问题，为集成感知与通信系统中的波束预测提供了一种数据高效且可靠的解决方案。

Abstract: Accurate beam prediction is essential for mitigating signalling overhead and latency in integrated sensing and communication-enabled massive multi-input multi-output systems. With the aid of multimodal learning, the prediction accuracy can be enhanced by leveraging the complementary information from other existing sensors, but the practical deployment is often constrained by the high cost of acquiring semantically aligned multimodal datasets. This paper proposes a variational-inference-based multimodal framework that decouples the optimization problem into modular feature extraction and cross-modal semantic alignment. Specifically, we develop a two-stage training strategy where the model utilises abundant unimodal data for representation learning before performing refined alignment on limited multimodal samples. This design enhances data efficiency and ensures robust feature fusion under sensing uncertainties. Experimental results on the DeepSense6G dataset demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves competitive beam prediction accuracy and maintains high reliability, while only requiring 20% of the multimodal training data compared to conventional end-to-end benchmarks.

### [2] [An integration-free approach for particle flow filtering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14852)
*Domonkos Csuzdi,Tamás Bécsi,Olivér Törő*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文针对对数同伦粒子流滤波器中的数值积分难题，提出了一种精确、无积分的闭式解。通过将ODE变换到特征空间，推导出与卡尔曼更新等价的解析表达式，并进一步通过N步切片法扩展到强非线性模型，有效缓解了刚度问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 对数同伦粒子流滤波器在实际应用中面临数值积分的计算成本高和刚度敏感问题，亟需一种无需积分的解决方案来提升效率和稳定性。

Method: 将伪时间常微分方程变换至特定特征空间，解析推导齐次状态转移矩阵和非齐次强迫项的闭式代数表达式，实现精确的无积分计算。

Result: 证明该解析解在数学上等价于精确卡尔曼测量更新，并可嵌入N步切片法中，为强非线性测量模型提供抗刚性的无积分粒子更新。

Conclusion: 该闭式解显著降低了计算成本，克服了传统方法的刚度缺陷，为线性和高度非线性测量模型下的贝叶斯估计提供了精确、高效的理论框架。

Abstract: Log-homotopy particle flow filters realize nonlinear Bayesian estimation by continuously migrating samples from the prior to the posterior distribution. This transport is governed by a pseudo-time ordinary differential equation (ODE). A major practical challenge of these filters is the need for numerical integration, which suffers from high computational cost and susceptibility to stiffness. This paper develops an exact, integration-free closed-form solution for the exact Daum--Huang (EDH) deterministic particle flow under vector linear Gaussian measurements. By transforming the ODE into a specific eigenspace, closed-form algebraic expressions are derived for both the homogeneous state transition matrix and the inhomogeneous forcing term. We prove that this analytic solution is mathematically equivalent to the exact Kalman measurement update. Furthermore, we demonstrate how this closed-form evaluation can be embedded within an $N$-step slicing method, providing a stiffness-mitigating, integration-free particle update for highly nonlinear measurement models.
