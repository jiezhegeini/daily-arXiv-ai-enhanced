### [1] [Complex Approximate Message Passing with Non-separable Denoising](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21115)
*Vishnu Teja Kunde,Alessandro Mirri,Jean-Francois Chamberland,Enrico Paolini*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文建立了复值非可分去噪函数下的状态演化理论，通过构造增广实值系统将问题提升到高维空间，再经多对一规范变换恢复复值域，使Onsager修正自然引入Wirtinger导数，并将状态演化简化为标量复值递归，同时扩展至矩阵值设定以处理多特征向量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尽管矩阵AMP、复值AMP及非可分函数AMP已有独立进展，但缺乏统一的复值非可分去噪器状态演化理论，限制了其在复值恢复问题中利用联合结构约束（如组稀疏与元素稀疏）的能力。

Method: 构造增广实值系统将复值问题升维至实值空间，通过多对一规范变换映射回复值域；在此框架下推导含Wirtinger导数的Onsager修正项，建立状态演化方程；进一步推广至矩阵值场景以同时处理多个特征向量。

Result: 成功建立复值非可分去噪的状态演化理论，可简化为标量复值递归；扩展至矩阵值情形支持多特征向量联合处理；在复值稀疏组LASSO问题（OTFS无源随机接入前导检测）中验证了状态演化的预测准确性，并显著优于可分及实值替代方案。

Conclusion: 该工作填补了复值非可分AMP状态演化的理论空白，为复值高维推断问题提供了统一框架，特别适用于需利用联合结构约束的复杂场景，并在无线通信等应用中展现出显著性能优势。

Abstract: Approximate Message Passing (AMP) is a general framework for iterative algorithms, originally developed for compressed sensing and later extended to a wide range of high-dimensional inference problems. Although recent work has advanced matrix AMP, complex AMP, and AMP for non-separable functions independently, a unified state evolution theory for complex AMP with non-separable denoisers has been lacking. This article fills that gap by establishing state evolution in the setting of complex, non-separable denoising functions. The proposed approach constructs an augmented real-valued system that lifts the problem to a higher-dimensional space, then recovers the complex domain through a many-to-one canonical transformation. Under this construction, the Onsager correction naturally involves Wirtinger derivatives, and the resulting state evolution reduces to scalar complex recursions despite the non-separable structure of the denoisers. The framework extends to the matrix-valued setting, accommodating multiple feature vectors simultaneously. This generalization enables AMP to exploit joint structural constraints, such as simultaneous group and element sparsity, in complex-valued recovery problems. The complex sparse group least absolute shrinkage and selection operator (LASSO) serves as a key instantiation, motivated by preamble detection in Orthogonal Time-Frequency Space (OTFS)-based unsourced random access. Numerical experiments confirm that state evolution accurately predicts performance and show that complex non-separable denoising can produce significant gains over separable and real-valued alternatives.

### [2] [Threat Detection and Resilience Techniques in PRS-Assisted OTDOA 5G Positioning Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21126)
*Thodoris Spanos,Nikolaos Papageorgiou,Samuele Fantinato,Nikos Kanistras,Sergi Duenas Pedrosa,Gianluca Caparra,Vassilis Paliouras*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对5G定位中DL-OTDOA的开放物理层安全漏洞，本文提出了VeriLoc仿真系统及三种安全增强技术：PRS加密、基于角度的源认证和跨层握手协议，通过实验证明这些方法能有效对抗欺骗、干扰和meaconing攻击，实现90%以上的检测率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 5G精准定位是自动驾驶和工业自动化的关键使能技术，但其开放的物理层使定位参考信号易受操纵。DL-OTDOA作为重要定位技术面临多种安全威胁，包括选择性欺骗、干扰和meaconing攻击，现有机制缺乏全面有效的防护手段。

Method: 1）开发开源系统级仿真平台VeriLoc，支持真实信道建模和物理层威胁注入；2）提出三种安全技术：加密PRS防止波形合成攻击、基于角度的源认证（ABSA）、以及跨层上下行握手协议；3）扩展HMAC认证方案支持数字签名；4）通过仿真评估各技术对选择性欺骗、干扰和meaconing攻击的防御效果。

Result: 仿真结果表明，加密、认证方案和位置追踪能有效对抗选择性PRS欺骗和干扰攻击；而提出的空间认证和跨层机制对检测meaconing攻击至关重要。综合使用这些技术可实现超过90%的攻击检测率，同时保持极低的误报率。

Conclusion: 该工作证明，结合加密认证、空间维度验证和跨层协作的多层次防御体系能有效保障5G定位安全，其中空间认证和跨层机制对应对复杂攻击（如meaconing）不可或缺，为未来5G/6G定位安全提供了重要参考。

Abstract: Precise positioning is a key enabler for emerging 5G applications, from autonomous transport to industrial automation. Yet the open physical layer (PL) leaves standard positioning reference signals (PRSs) vulnerable to manipulation. This work addresses the security of downlink observed time difference of arrival positioning (DL-OTDOA) through three contributions. First, we introduce VeriLoc, an open-source system-level simulator designed for realistic channel modeling and PL threat injection. Second, we propose three novel security techniques to enhance resilience and threat detection: encrypted PRS to prevent adversarial waveform synthesis, angular-based source authentication (ABSA), and a cross-layer downlink-uplink handshaking protocol to detect attacks that cannot be mitigated by encryption. Third, utilizing VeriLoc, we evaluate the proposed techniques alongside position tracking and a PRS authentication scheme, which extends the original hash-based message authentication code (HMAC) scheme design to support digital signatures. Simulation results demonstrate that while encryption, authentication schemes, and tracking robustly counter selective PRS spoofing and jamming, the proposed spatial and cross-layer mechanisms are essential for detecting meaconing, collectively maintaining attack detection rates in excess of 90% while keeping false alarm rates minimal.

### [3] [A Hidden Markov Framework for Physically Interpretable Arc Stability Dynamics in Welding Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21839)
*Hidir Selcuk Nogay*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本研究提出一种基于短时傅里叶变换（STFT）和隐马尔可夫模型（HMM）的概率状态空间框架，用于建模电弧焊接的非稳态动态行为，识别出瞬态、稳定和熄灭三种主导状态，并展示能量单调增加、熵单调减少以及强时间一致性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 电弧焊接（EAW）具有强烈的非稳态和时变特性，传统的基于帧的方法难以可靠评估电弧稳定性，因此需要能够捕捉时序演化的建模方法。

Method: 将焊接电流信号经短时傅里叶变换（STFT）转换到时频域，提取能量、熵和质心等物理意义明确的谱描述符作为观测序列，采用隐马尔可夫模型（HMM）捕捉时间依赖关系并估计电弧状态的演化。

Result: 分析揭示三种主导状态：瞬态、稳定和熄灭；在稳定状态下，谱能量单调增加、熵单调降低，表明变异性减小；状态序列表现出高持久性和低转移率，显示出强时间一致性，突显了时序建模的重要性。

Conclusion: 该框架提供了可解释且物理一致的电弧行为表征，使焊接过程的稳定性监测与分析更加真实可靠。

Abstract: Electric arc welding (EAW) exhibits strongly non stationary and temporally evolving behavior, making reliable assessment of arc stability difficult using conventional frame based approaches. In this study, arc dynamics are modeled as a sequence of latent operational regimes within a probabilistic state-space framework. The welding current signal is transformed into a time-frequency domain using Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT), and a set of physically meaningful spectral descriptors, including energy, entropy, and centroid, is extracted to construct the observation sequence. A Hidden Markov Model (HMM) is employed to capture temporal dependencies and estimate the evolution of arc states. The analysis reveals three dominant regimes, transient, stable, and extinction, with a clear monotonic increase in spectral energy and a corresponding decrease in entropy, indicating reduced variability under stable conditions. Despite partial overlap in the feature space, the inferred state sequence exhibits strong temporal coherence, supported by high state persistence and low transition rates. These findings highlight the limitations of static classification and emphasize the importance of temporal modeling. The proposed framework provides an interpretable and physically consistent representation of arc behavior, enabling more realistic monitoring and analysis of stability dynamics in welding processes.
