### [1] [Noise-Domain OTFS: Statistical Delay-Doppler Signaling for Future Wireless Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13557)
*Mahmoud Aldababsa*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 该论文提出了“噪声域 OTFS”（ND-OTFS），通过在延迟-多普勒域中利用随机向量的协方差而非确定性符号来编码信息，为 OTFS 通信系统提供了一种创新的统计信令维度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的 OTFS 系统依赖于在延迟-多普勒（DD）网格上放置确定性星座符号来传输信息。为了拓展 OTFS 的信息传输维度，研究人员探索是否可以通过在统计层面（即协方差域）进行编码，从而利用信号的统计属性来实现信息传输或增强系统性能。

Method: 提出了一种“噪声域 OTFS”（ND-OTFS）框架，其中信息通过随机延迟-多普勒（DD）域向量的协方差进行编码，而非传统的确定性星座符号。该方法涉及通过每箱方差、等能量相关结构或可分离的 DD 协方差来构建统计码字，并探讨了相应的收发机原理、接收机含义以及可靠性与速率的权衡。

Result: 研究表明，匹配有效载荷的相干 OTFS 在原始可靠性上保持优势；ND-OTFS 能够在竞争状态具有相同平均能量的情况下，通过协方差几何结构恢复信息。此外，通过额外的统计观测可以减少有限样本重叠，但会以牺牲延迟和速率为代价。

Conclusion: ND-OTFS 并非要取代传统的确定性 OTFS，而是通过引入延迟-多普勒域中的矩阵值统计信令维度，为其提供了一种互补的新视角，特别是在需要利用协方差几何结构进行信息恢复的场景中具有应用潜力。

Abstract: Orthogonal time frequency space (OTFS) usually conveys information through deterministic constellation symbols placed on a delay--Doppler (DD) grid. This article develops a different viewpoint, termed \emph{noise-domain OTFS} (ND-OTFS), in which each message selects the covariance of a random DD-domain vector. The resulting statistical codeword can encode information through per-bin variance, equal-energy correlation structure, or separable DD covariance. We explain the transceiver principle, receiver implications, reliability--rate tradeoffs, and practical challenges. Representative results show that a matched-payload coherent OTFS reference retains a clear raw-reliability advantage, whereas ND-OTFS enables information recovery from covariance geometry even when competing states have identical average energy. Additional statistical observations reduce finite-sample overlap at the cost of latency and rate. ND-OTFS therefore complements, rather than replaces, deterministic OTFS by introducing a matrix-valued statistical signaling dimension across DD resources.

### [2] [Combating Suppressive Jamming with Dynamic Agile Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface Antenna Array (DARISAA)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14511)
*Yakun Ma,Qingli Yan,Hui-Ming Wang*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于动态敏捷可重构智能表面天线阵列（DARISAA）的抗干扰方案，通过在天线前端进行空间域预处理，解决了高功率干扰导致ADC过载的问题，并在低分辨率ADC环境下显著提高了系统的信干噪比。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 高功率抑制性干扰会导致数字接收机ADC过载或AGC量化阻塞，从而掩盖微弱的目标信号。现有的数字域处理在干扰过强时往往失效，因此亟需一种能在模数转换前通过空间域模拟波束形成来抑制干扰的解决方案。

Method: 提出了一种动态敏捷可重构智能表面天线阵列（DARISAA）；利用DARISAA的动态敏捷性，结合子空间方法实现干扰与目标信号的DoA精确估计；利用DARISAA的空间预处理能力与多通道接收机的数字波束形成技术，构建了模拟-数字混合抗干扰架构。

Result: 仿真结果表明，该综合抗干扰方案显著提升了信干噪比（SINR），有效地解决了抑制性干扰问题，证明了DARISAA架构在天线前端处理中的优势。

Conclusion: 本文提出的基于DARISAA的模拟-数字混合抗干扰方案，通过在天线端进行空间预处理，能够有效应对高功率抑制性干扰，即使在低分辨率ADC条件下也能显著提升系统性能，验证了DARISAA在复杂电磁环境下的抗干扰潜力。

Abstract: Suppressive jamming is a severe challenge to digital receivers in wireless communications, since high-power jamming may cause analog-to-digital converter (ADC) overload or automatic gain control (AGC)-limited quantization blocking of weak desired signals. Analog beamforming in the spatial domain can be employed to combat suppressive jamming signals before they reach the ADC. In this paper, we introduce a comprehensive anti-jamming scheme considering both direction-of-arrival (DoA) acquisition and suppressive jamming elimination based on a Dynamic Agile Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface Antenna Array (DARISAA). DARISAA is a novel type of reconfigurable antenna array composed of many metamaterial elements, which can dynamically adapt its phase responses, enabling suppressive jamming cancellation at the antenna-end. Accurate DoA estimation schemes for jamming and desired signals are proposed based on a subspace approach by utilizing the dynamic agility of the DARISAA. By leveraging the spatial-domain preprocessing capability of DARISAA and the digital beamforming of the multi-channel receiver, an analog (antenna)-digital hybrid anti-jamming scheme is developed to effectively suppress high-power jamming and significantly improve the signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) even with low-resolution ADCs. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed comprehensive anti-jamming scheme achieves significant SINR enhancement, highlighting the advantages of DARISAA-enabled spatial front-end processing for jamming suppression.

### [3] [HELENA for 5G NR LEO NTN Channel Estimation: A Comparative Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14735)
*Miguel Camelo Botero,Nina Slamnik-Kriještorac,Johann Marquez-Barja*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本研究验证了 HELENA 模型在非地面网络（NTN）信道估计中的有效性，证明了其无需针对 NTN 进行架构 redesign 即可取得优于现有 NTN 专用模型的精度，并揭示了嵌入式设备在满足实时延迟指标方面仍面临严峻挑战。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的基于深度学习的信道估计主要针对地面 5G 网络，而低轨卫星（LEO）非地面网络（NTN）面临独特的 Doppler 频移和同步损伤问题。本研究旨在探讨已有的高性能深度学习信道估计架构（HELENA）是否无需修改架构，仅通过重新训练即可有效应用于 NTN 环境，并评估其在不同推理平台上的性能表现。

Method: 研究人员采用 HELENA 模型，在包含接收机补偿数据（NTN-1）和残余损伤数据（NTN-2）的 NTN 特定数据集上进行训练。通过与八种其他地面原始模型以及专门为 NTN 设计的 MDELAN-SISO 模型进行对比，评估其性能。同时，研究在 RTX PRO 4500（高性能）和 Jetson Orin NX（功耗受限）两个平台上对该模型的推理延迟和能量效率进行了实测。

Result: 在 NTN 环境下，HELENA 在所有对比模型中实现了最低的平均均方误差（NMSE），在线性尺度上比 MDELAN-SISO 降低了 55.8%-62.7%。在高性能 RTX PRO 4500 平台上，HELENA 的推理延迟远低于 0.5 ms 的预算，但在 Jetson Orin NX 平台上，所有模型均未能在 P99 延迟指标下满足实时性要求。

Conclusion: HELENA 模型无需针对非地面网络（NTN）进行架构重构即可表现出色。研究表明，利用现有的高效深度学习模型进行针对性训练，足以应对 NTN 环境下的信道估计任务。然而，在功耗受限的嵌入式边缘计算平台（如 Jetson Orin NX）上，实现严格的实时性要求仍是未来的关键挑战。

Abstract: Deep Learning (DL)-based channel estimation has shown high accuracy and low latency in terrestrial 5G NR, but Low Earth Orbit (LEO) Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTNs) introduce Doppler and synchronization impairments that may require NTN-specific architectures. We test whether High-Efficiency Learning-based channel Estimation using dual Neural Attention (HELENA), originally designed for terrestrial channels, remains effective after NTN retraining and suitable across high-performance and power-constrained inference platforms. Its unchanged architecture is trained on paired receiver-compensated (NTN-1) and residual-impaired (NTN-2) datasets and compared with eight terrestrial-origin models trained on the same NTN data and the NTN-specific MDELAN-SISO. HELENA achieves the lowest observed SNR-averaged NMSE among the DL estimators in both conditions, including 55.8-62.7% lower linear-scale NMSE than MDELAN-SISO. All DL models degrade in NTN-2, demonstrating the challenge posed by residual Doppler and its associated impairments. On an RTX PRO 4500, HELENA achieves 0.0595 ms 99th-percentile (P99) inference latency, 88.1% below the 0.5 ms budget, with lower energy than its closest attention-based competitors. On a 10 W Jetson Orin NX, it retains a favorable accuracy-energy trade-off, but no model meets the P99 budget. Thus, HELENA needs no NTN-specific redesign for the evaluated task, while embedded tail latency remains an open challenge.

### [4] [Full-Wave-Calibrated Element-Wise RIS Modeling With Cross-Aperture Coefficient Transfer for Multipath Channel Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15173)
*Yuxuan Ding,Minseok Kim*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对实际RIS中未被理想模型捕获的寄生散射问题，本文提出了一种基于布拉格级数迁移的全波校准模型，在大幅降低大规模RIS仿真计算成本的同时，显著提升了在复杂多径环境下的路径增益预测精度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 实际的可重构智能表面（RIS）存在确定性的寄生散射现象，而理想的逐元素模型无法描述这一现象，导致对RIS辅助路径增益的过高估计及多径预测偏差。因此，需要开发一种既能捕捉实际散射特性，又能在大规模阵列中高效部署的校准模型。

Method: 1. 提出了一种基于三个布拉格级数基函数的全波校准元素级模型，用以捕获预期反射及主要的寄生散射；2. 利用射线追踪（RT）技术识别RIS与接收端（Rx）之间的反射序列，并通过镜像理论将其展开为路径相关的镜像点；3. 采用系数迁移策略，从25x25的小型校准孔径中提取布拉格系数，应用于更大规模的RIS阵列（如50x50和100x100），从而大幅降低全波校准的计算负担。

Result: 在154 GHz频率下，通过系数迁移，50x50和100x100 RIS阵列的预期阶数误差控制在0.8 dB以内；相比直接全波校准，计算时间从7.56小时缩短至1.08小时；在多径验证中，该模型将室内环境下的预测误差从6.49 dB降低至0.23 dB，显著优于未校准的通用模型。

Conclusion: 该研究提出了一种基于布拉格级数校准的RIS建模方法，能够以极低的计算开销实现高精度的全波级建模。通过从较小规模的校准孔径迁移系数，研究有效解决了大规模RIS阵列在实际复杂环境中的多径预测偏差问题，并显著提升了仿真效率。

Abstract: Practical reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RISs) can exhibit deterministic parasitic scattering that is not captured by idealized element-wise models. As a result, such models may overestimate the gain of the intended RIS-assisted path and bias multipath prediction. This paper develops a full-wave-calibrated element-wise model using three Bragg-order basis functions to represent the intended and dominant parasitic scattering components. Environmental multipath is incorporated by identifying RIS--Rx reflection sequences with ray tracing (RT) and unfolding them by image theory into path-dependent image points. This allows direct and reflected RIS-assisted paths to be evaluated using the same per-element kernel and coherently combined with Tx--Rx bypass paths. To reduce the full-wave calibration burden for large RISs under a prescribed focusing configuration, only the three Bragg-order coefficients are transferred from a 25-by-25 calibration aperture, while the target-aperture basis functions and geometry are recomputed. At 154 GHz, the transferred coefficients keep the intended-order errors within 0.8 dB for the 50-by-50 and 100-by-100 RISs, while reducing the full-wave calibration time for the prescribed configuration from 7.56 to 1.08 h relative to direct 100-by-100 calibration. For multipath validation with the 50-by-50 RIS, the calibrated model using the same transferred coefficients keeps the nominal-Rx gain error within 0.88 dB across four PEC reflector configurations, compared with 1.75--5.51 dB for the uncalibrated general model, and reduces the error from 6.49 to 0.23 dB in the scaled indoor environment.

</details>
