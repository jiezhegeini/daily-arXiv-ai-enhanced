### [1] [Efficient Channel Prediction based on Gram-Square-Root Factorization using GMMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26959)
*Kathrin Klein,Amar Kasibovic,Michael Joham,Shachar Shayovitz,Wolfgang Utschick*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于高斯混合模型（GMM）和Gram平方根分解的信道预测方案，在有限反馈约束下，通过高效的参数约减技术，显著提升了MIMO-OFDM系统的信道预测准确度，达到了与全信息反馈相当的性能。

Motivation: 在下行多用户MIMO系统中，由于移动性和反馈延迟，信道状态信息（CSI）的准确性受到影响。此外，由于系统开销限制，获取完整CSI通常不可行，因此需要一种能在部分反馈条件下高效预测CSI的方法，以保障波束赋形和干扰抑制的性能。

Method: 提出了一种基于高斯混合模型（GMM）的MIMO-OFDM信道预测框架，引入了基于Gram平方根因式分解（Gram-square-root factorization）的参数约减技术，利用结构化协方差矩阵降低高维计算复杂度。

Result: 仿真结果表明，该GMM预测模型在预测精度上优于零阶保持（ZOH）、一阶保持（FOH）、线性最小均方误差（LMMSE）以及先进的神经网络（NN）方法；且通过参数约减后的部分反馈GMM方案，其性能可与全CSI预测相当。

Conclusion: 本文提出了一种基于GMM和Gram平方根因式分解的信道预测框架，通过高效的参数约减技术，实现了在部分反馈条件下接近全信道状态信息（Full CSI）的预测精度，证明了该方法在MIMO-OFDM系统中处理动态信道与有限反馈问题的优越性。

Abstract: Accurate channel state information (CSI) is critical for downlink (DL)-multi-user (MU)-multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) systems, where feedback delays and mobility can degrade precoding performance. To ensure reliable beamforming and interference mitigation, CSI prediction is required. In practical systems, full CSI feedback is often infeasible due to signaling overhead, so transmitters rely on partial CSI reported by the receivers. In this work, we propose a Gaussian mixture model (GMM)-based prediction framework for MIMO-orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing (OFDM) channels under partial feedback using Gram-square-root factorization. To address the high dimensionality, we introduce an efficient parameter reduction technique that exploits structured covariance matrices, significantly lowering complexity without noticeable performance degradation. This reduction is based on the Gram-square-root factorization and remains of interest even when full CSI is available. Simulation results demonstrate that GMMs achieve the highest prediction accuracy and correctly capture the underlying channel subspaces, which is essential for effective MU-precoding. The proposed method outperforms classical baselines such as zero-order hold (ZOH), first-order hold (FOH), and linear minimum mean squared error (LMMSE) predictors, and an advanced neural network (NN)-based predictor. Notably, the parameter-reduced partial CSI GMM achieves performance comparable to that of full CSI prediction, highlighting its ability to efficiently model the channel structure under limited feedback.
